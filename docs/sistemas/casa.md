# Casa propia and the neighbours' houses

> Moved from the root `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-11, word for word. Keep it in sync with the code; the root keeps only a pointer here.

**Casa propia** (JJ): a yellow arrow in front of the first Urbi house (`urbi_1p_a`, `CasaPropia` node, `scripts/interiores/CasaPropia.gd`) enters `scenes/casa_personaje.tscn`, the protagonist's own house. Unlike the shops (`Interiores.gd`: fixed first-person scenes) you walk it as the real `Player`: it fades to black, instances the house `ALTURA` 500 m above the arrow (its own `Ambiente` WorldEnvironment is freed so the yard's rules), puts you inside the door facing east and turns `Zombis.espontaneos` off (restored on leaving; zombies already out vanish by `_quitar_lejanos`). **The house is the place you wake up after fainting, not a fighting place** (JJ, 2026-11-10): inside, `Player.poner_sin_combate(true)` puts the weapons away and sets `Player.sin_combate`, which blocks swings, thrusts, rolls (`_pedir_accion` lets only `brinco` through), weapon switching (`cambiar_arma`, the `cambiar_arma` key), healing (`_curarse`), blocking and all damage (`recibir_dano`), hides the weapon in hand (`_mostrar_arma`), and `Supervivencia._cambio_vida` sets life back to 1 instead of fainting; walking, jumping and looking still work. Waking up there after a faint isn't wired yet (`Supervivencia._desmayarse` still uses `punto_regreso`), collectibles inside come later. **In the Blender map** (AG, 2026-11-10) the house is the family's rooms in Santa Mugre's vecindad: the marker `casa_propia` makes `Colonia.gd` build a `CasaPropia` there, with `CasaPropia.muebles` = `scripts/interiores/MueblesCasa.gd`, a node hung on the house when you enter. It finds JJ's furniture by node name (so his builder can regenerate the scene) and gives each an `interactuable` `MuebleCasa` area with a prompt:
  - `Cama`: sleep; `Ciclo.saltar(true)` lands past the transition, and life fills.
  - `Closet`: `CreadorPersonaje`.
  - `Vitrina`: the trophy case (`TilichesUI` with `vitrina`).
  - `Mesa`: the album; `Sillon`: the libreta, both via the new `InventoryUI.abrir_pagina("album"|"libreta"|"mapa")`.
  - `Tele2`: GelA news lines.
  - The neighbours' houses (Santa Mugre doc, houses 7-10) are entered the same way: marker `casa_vecino` (from `vecinos` in the colonia JSON, placed by `bloqueo._vecinos` like the shops) -> a `CasaPropia` with `scenes/casas/<quien>.tscn`, built by `godot --path . --headless --script res://tools/construir_casas_vecinos.gd` (one 6 x 5 m room, door where JJ's is so his entry/exit points fit, his textures and Sketchfab furniture). `CasaPropia.abierto` ("siempre"/"noche"/"dia") keeps Sofía's workshop shut by day. `scripts/interiores/CasaVecino.gd` on each root puts the occupant (`npc.tscn` with a `Civiles` model and lines cycling through `al_hablar`) and the action: the Güero talks, Sofía's radio, Toño sells a mazapán, the GelA-closed house has a seal and loot once per run (`Baul.saqueadas`). Their real missions come later. The interactable is `scripts/interiores/MuebleCasa.gd` (`MuebleCasa.crear`).
  - New props: a chest (`Baul` autoload, run-scoped, reset by `Partida._otra_vez`; `BaulUI.gd`, two columns, Enter moves one unit), an altar to the Virgen del Tinaco, stacked garrafones, and the paper map on the wall (Santa Mugre cropped from `minimapa.png`; opens `MapaUI`). You can go in at night. You leave by stepping on the yellow arrow beside its door, which puts you in front of the street arrow. The scene is generated, not hand-edited: `godot --path . --headless --script res://tools/construir_casa_personaje.gd` (layout, window and door grilles, closed kitchen and bathroom doors, TV table) with textures from `python3 -I tools/make_texturas_casa.py` (plaster walls and ceiling, tile floor); `scripts/CasaPersonaje.gd` tints the table to the chairs' wood on load (Godot doesn't save material changes on imported .glb meshes). Its 7 Sketchfab models (`models/casa_personaje/`, credited in `CREDITS.md`) were slimmed from 44 MB to 5 MB with `python3 -I tools/aligerar_glb.py models/casa_personaje 512` (colour maps only, 512 px JPG, matte; imported embedded, no extracted textures).
