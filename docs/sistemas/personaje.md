# Character (`scripts/Personaje.gd`)

> Moved from the root `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-11, word for word. Keep it in sync with the code; the root keeps only a pointer here.

`Personaje` autoload: protagonist, second nickname (`nombre`: the hero is Marcial López, `docs/lore.md` §15, though nobody knows it; people call him "Machetes" (`APODO`) or the nickname you pick, at random, through `Personaje.apodo()`; empty means only "Machetes", and "Al azar" picks from `NOMBRES`), skin tone, build, height, head and clothes, saved in `user://personaje.cfg` and kept on restart. **Looks** (`Personaje.PROTAGONISTAS`, the creator's first row "Look", shown as "N de 5" with no names: JJ, 2026-11-12, they are only looks of the one protagonist, Marcial "el Machetes"; the names below stay internal, in code and folders): "Genaro" (the plain el_compa model; the folder and model keep the el_compa name), "Homero", "Ramón", "Emiliano" and "Felipe" (Ramón: black short-sleeved tee, blue jeans, dark sneakers, no mustache, painted by `el_compa.gd -- --ramon` into `protagonistas/ramon/`, plus a blue bucket hat that isn't texture: `scripts/personajes/GorroPescador.gd` builds one shared mesh in the `Head` bone's space (measured from the skin's bind pose: X is the width, the face looks +Z, crown at y 0.55) and `Personaje._poner_gorro` hangs it on a `BoneAttachment3D` only on el_compa models; `GORROS` holds each protagonist's hat script, or null. Emiliano, JJ's homage to Zapata: brown jacket with shirt cuffs, olive vest with buttons and a gold watch chain, white shirt, red neckerchief, dark grey trousers, black shoes and a big drooping mustache (`el_compa.gd -- --emiliano`, `protagonistas/emiliano/`), plus `SombreroCharro.gd`: a tall crown and a 0.86 brim rolled up at the edge, woven chevrons as flat per-face sRGB vertex colours). Felipe: an open long-sleeved plaid jacket (`_cuadro`, stripes along y and z + x in model space) with orange lining showing at the opening and the turned collar, a yellow tee, jeans, dark sneakers and a thin mustache (`el_compa.gd -- --felipe`, `protagonistas/felipe/`), no hat. **Fist fighting is coded but unused**: JJ first had Felipe brawl with powered punches, then gave him the machete back because a bare-handed character may be hard to balance; `Personaje.A_PUNOS` is all false, and setting one true brings back all of this (`Player._a_punos()`): the weapon is never shown (`_mostrar_arma`), every melee attack is the punch combo (`_punos()`), but each hit uses the best weapon's damage (`_golpear`: `con_arma` stays false, so the hook's 1.6× finisher applies too), and `_especiales()` unlocks for him what plain bare hands don't get: charge and thrust (`Sword_Dash_RM` without a sword), the aerial spin and attacking in the air. His punches leave a trail (`ESTELA_PUNOS` windows; the jab from the left hand, `_mano_izq`, `ESTELA_IZQUIERDA`), and with no `_punta` every trail (punches, thrust, spin) runs from `ESTELA_MUNECA` behind the fist to `ESTELA_PUNO` past the knuckles, passed to `EstelaBarrido.agregar(..., a_puno)` so it's even instead of starting part-way and dropping its inner edge like the sword's. A straight punch moves along the wrist-knuckle line, so that ribbon was a sliver: for punches `_estela_puno` lays the ribbon across instead (`PUNO_ANCHO` 0.7 m, perpendicular to the fist's motion and the camera ray), dragging a band the length of the punch, and at each punch's hit time (`_golpear`) the fist bursts: with the bolt trail, `PUNO_ESTALLIDO` 3 bolts `ESTALLIDO_ADELANTE` 0.3 m ahead; with any other trail (Felipe's fire), `PUNO_LLAMAS` 7 clouds at `PUNO_LLAMAS_ESCALA` 1.8× shot toward the punch (`EstelaBarrido.estallar(donde, n, hacia, escala)`: per-cloud velocity braked by `ESTALLIDO_FRENA`; the pool is `NUBES` 32), plus `_fogonazo`, a light in the trail's colour that fades over `FOGONAZO_DURA` (JJ: punches should feel as strong as the machete). Genaro and Homero are (JJ's homage to Homero Gómez, the monarch butterfly defender), the same el_compa model with another texture: `_pintar_piel` swaps `TEXTURA_COMPA` for `TEXTURAS[protagonista]` (`models/personajes/protagonistas/homero/Character_04.png` with its own `piel.png`, painted by `tools/personajes/el_compa.gd -- --homero`: closed white long-sleeved guayabera worn outside the trousers, drawn in model space like the mustache, with pleats, four pockets and buttons, black trousers and shoes). Each has its own trail upgrade (`Personaje.ESTELAS`, applied by `Player.aplicar_personaje` to `EstelaBarrido.estilo`): Genaro fire, Homero the monarch, Ramón water, Emiliano brown lightning, Felipe black fire (grey flames). It's off for all (`Personaje.mejora_estela` false, so the normal trail) until it becomes a damage upgrade (`docs/mejoras.md`). Every number and line is at the top of that script. `scripts/CreadorPersonaje.gd` is the screen, opened after Jugar (`Inicio._crear_personaje`) and by C in the safehouse. **Skin tone only changes how the world treats you**: `Interior._factor` (peso prices, not water), `Misiones._completar` (pesos, not items) and `Personaje.linea` (shop, givers, neighbours). Combat, zombie drops and fruit machines must stay out of it: that's the joke, and a darker character is never weaker. Build and height change life, speed, damage and reach in `Player.aplicar_personaje`, on top of the scene's own values. `Personaje.vestir` recolours one model's own copies of the ninja's `Skin` and `Main` materials or, for a PSX-pack model, its texture where the `piel.png` beside it is white (`_pintar_piel`: the tone times each pixel's brightness over the painted skin's average, one shared `ImageTexture` per texture, ~25 ms per change; clothes for these models come later, so the creator hides Ropa), and `scripts/CuerpoPersonaje.gd` (`SkeletonModifier3D`) scales bones after the animation; the collision capsule stays the same. This is AG's proposal; the team hasn't voted on it (spec §10.2).

## Protagonist model and retargeting

- **Protagonist and retargeting** (branch `protagonista-compa`): the player is `el_compa` (`models/personajes/protagonistas/el_compa/`, hombre_04 of the PSX pack repainted by `tools/personajes/el_compa.gd`), at scale 0.39 (~1.8 m), with Quaternius's UAL1/UAL2 clips (CC0). Every skeleton is retargeted on import to `SkeletonProfileHumanoid` (`retarget/bone_map` plus `fix_silhouette` in each `.import`'s `_subresources`; the Mixamo pack is T-pose and Quaternius A-pose, so without `fix_silhouette` the elbows flared): the bone maps are `resources/personajes/huesos_{mixamo,rigify,unreal}.tres`, written by `tools/personajes/mapas_huesos.gd`, and the result is a `%GeneralSkeleton` with profile bone names (`Hips`, `RightHand`...). **`find_child("Skeleton3D")` no longer finds it: search by class** (`find_children("*", "Skeleton3D")`). `tools/personajes/animaciones.gd` gathers the clips into `resources/personajes/animaciones_humanas.res` (dropping tracks for bones el_compa lacks); add a clip to `CLIPS` there and rerun, then `--import`. `character_animation.gd` builds an `AnimationTree` in code: feet (Idle/Jog_Fwd/Sprint/Walk_Carry/Jump, time-scaled by real speed), the pistol on the upper body only (Pistol_Idle, or a Pistol_Aim Down/Neutral/Up blend by aim height, kicked up per shot), a reload one-shot, a full-body action layer (the sword swings, the charge pose, Hit_Chest: a `Blend2` whose weight the script drives with `_accion_resta`/`_accion_peso`, not a `OneShot`, which ended at the first clip's length when the transition had already switched to the next swing, so every other mashed swing played with a frozen arm, and re-firing it snapped the arm to idle for a moment; swings hold their last frame `ACCION_QUEDA` 0.2 s so a late next swing starts from the follow-through, not from idle) and Death01 held on the floor. Transitions start with no state (T-pose) unless one is requested, which `_ready` does. The 41 civilians (`pack_psx/hombre_*`, `mujer_*`) get the same retarget (`tools/personajes/retarget_civiles.gd` writes it into their `.import`) and 40 of them are placed by `scripts/personajes/Civiles.gd` (`mujer_01` is left out: the pack has no texture for her and she rendered all white; static: `poner(padre, nombre, clip)` at scale 0.39 with the clip library `"h"`, `elegir(semilla)` for a fixed pick, `calentar()` threaded loads from `MapPuntos._calentar`; the scenes stay in a static cache, because without a held reference each `load()` re-read the model from disk, ~3.5 ms per seller while walking). Other NPCs still use the ninja (`models/ninja_male.fbx`, clips looked up by suffix because the FBX prefixes them).
