# Fighting: getting hit, melee, block, roll, pistol

> Moved from the root `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-11, word for word. Keep it in sync with the code; the root keeps only a pointer here.

- **Getting hit**: `Player.recibir_dano(dano, desde, empuja)` freezes, flashes the model white, shakes, pushes about 1.5 m away from the attacker (3 m from the boss), sprays blood the same way and emits `golpeado`, on which `Supervivencia` shows 2 frames of hard black and white (`shaders/impacto.gdshader`, 17 ms each on the real clock, `IMPACTO_ACTIVO`). All of this is limited by the 0.7 s invulnerability. `Supervivencia` always processes so the impact can't stick under the inventory.
- `Player`: `attack` action (left click, F, gamepad X, only while the mouse is captured; with it freed by Esc, a left click recaptures it without attacking) in a forward cone (`_golpear`), hitting only the nearest live zombie (`ATAQUE_MAXIMO` 1): in a packed group one combo used to kill 5. Damage is the best `InventoryItemData.dano` in the inventory (`Inventory.arma()`, machete 12, reinforced 18), or 5 bare-handed; nothing needs equipping, and the weapon shows in the hand (`RightHand`, turned by `ARMA_GIRO`: in that bone X is the thumb, Y the fingers, Z the palm, so the blade leaves the thumb side with the edge toward the fingers), `ARMA_TAMANO` 0.95 m long (0.6 read as a knife). `player.gd` is loaded by `tools/` scripts through `MapPuntos`, so it reaches autoloads with `get_node("/root/...")`. Its timers tick in `_physics_process`, so they stop while it's frozen. Only on the floor. **Action buffer** (JJ, Smash-style; `_pedir_accion`/`_probar_buffer`): swing, jump, roll and thrust pressed while something else won't allow it (`_ocupado()`: rolling, thrusting, spinning, healing; or `_barriendo()`: a swing before `_combo_desde()`, so a roll or jump during a swing waits for the end of the sweep instead of cutting it) are kept in one slot (`_buffer`, the latest wins) for `BUFFER_ACCION` 0.3 s of game time (`_buffer_edad`, so the hit freeze slows it too; wall-clock ms ran out early) and fire as soon as `_puede()` allows. Swing-to-swing keeps the short combo buffer (`BUFFER_GOLPE`). A jump or roll in the buffer beats a guarded combo swing when the sweep ends (mashing used to fire the swing first, which blocked the jump again until it expired); with `giro_aereo`, that swing becomes the air attack right after the jump (`_golpe_tras_brinco`: spin, or air thrust with Shift), so swing, swing, jump, spin chains with no pause.
