# Fighting: getting hit, melee, block, roll, pistol

> Moved from the root `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-11, word for word. Keep it in sync with the code; the root keeps only a pointer here.

- **Getting hit**: `Player.recibir_dano(dano, desde, empuja)` freezes, flashes the model white, shakes, pushes about 1.5 m away from the attacker (3 m from the boss), sprays blood the same way and emits `golpeado`, on which `Supervivencia` shows 2 frames of hard black and white (`shaders/impacto.gdshader`, 17 ms each on the real clock, `IMPACTO_ACTIVO`). All of this is limited by the 0.7 s invulnerability. `Supervivencia` always processes so the impact can't stick under the inventory.
- **Knocked flying** (JJ, 2026-11-23): a hit with `empuja` ≥ `VUELA_DESDE` 2 that isn't blocked (Simi kick 3, cucarrata bite 3 and sting 3.5, pterodactyl claws 2 and charge 3, Panzón hammer 2, Catrina stomp/sweep/charge/head 2, Madre del Cárcamo and the worm tower/ball) goes to `Player._salir_volando` instead of the push. You face the attacker and fly backwards: `VUELA_SUBE` 11 m/s up × √(empuja/2) (gravity 40: 1.5 m at 2, 2.3 m at 3) and `VUELA_LEJOS` 3.8 m/s per point of `empuja` sideways, braking only `VUELA_FRENO_AIRE` in the air (the Simi kick lands ~8 m away and slides to ~10). The upward speed is applied in `_physics_process` after gravity (`_vuelo_sube`), because the floor snap zeroed it. The animation comes from UAL2 Source clips added to the library: `LiftAir` (0.47 s), `LiftAir_Fall_Air` looping until `is_on_floor`, `LiftAir_Fall_Impact` (lands on the back, with a shake and a `VUELA_ARRASTRE` slide), lying `VUELA_TIRADO` 0.45 s, then `LayToIdle` 1.53 s. The poses chain end to start (measured). `character_animation.vuelo(fase)` puts the `estado` transition on `vuela`, a sub-transition with one `TimeSeek` per phase. While `_vuelo != ""` there's no control (input, `_ocupado`, jump, aim-facing) and `_invulnerable` is INF, then `INVULNERABLE` after getting up; `atrapar` refuses. If it gets stuck in the air, it lands anyway after `VUELA_MAXIMO` 4 s. Safehouse: Ctrl+J. `godot --path . --write-movie x.avi --fixed-fps 30 --resolution 640x480 tools/probar_vuelo.tscn` records it against each boss in turn, in the safehouse with the clutter removed. The player stands still in godmode while the wait runs at 2× and the flight plays in slow motion; `-- --prueba` only prints who sent you flying, after how long and how far (last recording: `~/capturas/vuelo_jefes.mp4`). The Mojiganga's throw also goes through it (`Catrina.LANZA_EMPUJA` 2.5). Quaternius also has `KipUp` (a spring-up from the same lying pose) and `Hit_Knockback` (a short fall on the back) if a faster or smaller version is ever wanted.

Its result only exists after the modifiers run: measure it in `Skeleton3D.skeleton_updated`, not in `_physics_process`. **Backpedal**: S (alone or diagonal) mid-swing or aiming walks backwards at `RETROCEDE_VELOCIDAD` 0.55 (then the cap), facing away from the movement; doing nothing else, S turns you and you jog toward the camera as before (JJ). **Roll** (`rodar`, `Player._rodar`): left Alt is a modifier: holding it, each WASD press rolls that way (`_es_direccion`), and pressing Alt with a direction already held rolls too; Alt alone does nothing. It cancels the combo and charge; attacking, shooting, healing and jumping are blocked meanwhile, and there's `RODAR_ENFRIA` 0.4 s before the next one. Only on the floor. **Action buffer** (JJ, Smash-style; `_pedir_accion`/`_probar_buffer`): swing, jump, roll and thrust pressed while something else won't allow it (`_ocupado()`: rolling, thrusting, spinning, healing; or `_barriendo()`: a swing before `_combo_desde()`, so a roll or jump during a swing waits for the end of the sweep instead of cutting it) are kept in one slot (`_buffer`, the latest wins) for `BUFFER_ACCION` 0.3 s of game time (`_buffer_edad`, so the hit freeze slows it too; wall-clock ms ran out early) and fire as soon as `_puede()` allows. A held attack doesn't expire: when free it goes straight to the charge pose with the held time counting (if it's still held but under `CARGA_DECIDE` when the action frees, `_probar_buffer` waits until it reaches it, pose, or is released, swing; the slash frees 0.31 s after release, so otherwise a hold pressed late in it gave a swing first), or, released charged meanwhile, queues the thrust. Swing-to-swing keeps the short combo buffer (`BUFFER_GOLPE`). A jump or roll in the buffer beats a guarded combo swing when the sweep ends (mashing used to fire the swing first, which blocked the jump again until it expired); with `giro_aereo`, that swing becomes the air attack right after the jump (`_golpe_tras_brinco`: spin, or air thrust with Shift), so swing, swing, jump, spin chains with no pause.
