# Throwables: salt, Molotov, palomita, bocina, gasolinera

> Moved from the root `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-11, word for word. Keep it in sync with the code; the root keeps only a pointer here. The code is in `scripts/lombrices/` (`Lanzador`, `Arrojadizo`, `ZonaLombrices`...), but zombies and the map use it too.

- **Salt is a handful** (JJ, 2026-11-09): with salt in hand (the machete is hidden while the `Lanzador` is active, `_mostrar_arma`; it looked like a ghost sword), a click calls `Player.aventar_sal(dir)`: the player turns to where the camera looks, stays put (`_aventando`, part of `_ocupado`; getting hit doesn't play `RecieveHit` meanwhile, so the throw always finishes) and plays `Sword_Heavy_A` (character_animation entry `aventar`), which JJ picked from the UAL2 **Source** tier: `models/animaciones/quaternius_ual2_source/UAL2_Source.glb` (CC0, 20 MB, copied from the nooneisreal project, retargeted like the Standard one and listed last in `animaciones.gd`'s `FUENTES`, so it only adds clips the others lack). Measured on el_compa, the hand goes almost all the way round between 0.38 and 0.57 s, crossing the front left to right around 0.40-0.53 (at ~0.85 m, up to 30 m/s), still from 0.63. **Only in front, following the hand** (JJ): every frame of `AVIENTA_ESTELA` `Player._arco_sal` takes the hand's angle from the facing (flattened), clamps the slice it swept since last frame to `AVIENTA_ARCO` 75° each side (skipping the wrap behind), salts that slice out to `Lanzador.PUNADO_ALCANCE` 3.3 m (`salar_tramo` → `EnjambreLombrices.salar_cono`, whose `ya` dictionary, shared by the whole throw, salts each 12-dot block once; `PUNADO_COME` 0 means every cría reached dies whole on the spot, ball members included (JJ), while a positive `come` eats that many dots from the head, pushes it back and takes 2× `BOLA_SAL` from a ball; latched ones always burn) and lays the same slice of a white smoke arc every `AVIENTA_PASO` 5°: an `EstelaBarrido` with `humo` true (the sword trail's crescent shape with `SHADER_HUMO`: soft transparent smoke, two fbm noises scrolling along it, white to grey, thinning from the inner edge and fading over `HUMO_VIDA` 0.55 s; JJ didn't want dots), which every `HUMO_NUBE_CADA` 2 pieces releases a soft smoke cloud (`SHADER_NUBE_HUMO`, an fbm billboard puff, not pixel art) drifting outward at `HUMO_NUBE_SALE` 0.4-1.8 m/s, growing `HUMO_NUBE_TAMANO` 0.45-1.4 m and fading over 0.9 s, from `AVIENTA_HUMO` 0.9 m (0.1 m under the hand) to 3.1 m (0.55 m under, toward the floor). `fizz` plays at `AVIENTA_SUELTA` 0.45; you can move again at `AVIENTA_DURA` 0.66. No aiming guide (JJ). Nothing is left on the floor (measured headless: 5 of 5 crías within reach killed whole). The Molotov is charged and thrown (below).
- `Lanzador` (child of Player, key 3 = `cambiar_arma(2)`, 3 again toggles salt/Molotov) throws from inventory items with `InventoryItemData.arrojar` (1 sal, 2 molotov). **The Molotov is charged** (JJ, 2026-11-09): pressing attack starts `_cargando`; while held, the landing point (`_destino_carga`) advances along the camera's flattened forward from `CARGA_DESDE` 3 m to `ALCANCE` 16 m over `CARGA_DURA` 1.2 s (stopped short of walls, dropped to the floor by a ray), and the floor shows the area it will burn (a ring and a translucent orange disc of `Arrojadizo.FUEGO_RADIO`, yellow once fully charged) with the parabola in dots. Releasing (`soltar_clic`, from `Player`'s `attack` release) takes the item and calls `Player.aventar_molotov(dir)`: the player faces that way and plays `OverhandThrow` (character_animation entry `molotov`; measured: wind-up to 0.27 s, release over the shoulder at `MOLOTOV_SUELTA` 0.33, free at `MOLOTOV_DURA` 0.6), sharing `_aventando` with the salt (so it's in `_ocupado` and hits don't interrupt it), and at the release `Lanzador.soltar_molotov` throws the `Arrojadizo` from the hand along the parabola to `_destino`.
- **Molotov fire** (JJ, 2026-11-10): `ZonaLombrices` FUEGO no longer uses the pixel `fuego_tambo` sheet. `_llamas` builds an oval, irregular stain (a rotated ellipse with sine bumps, never past `radio`, so the damage circle still covers it; `mancha_fuego.gdshader`: charred centre, frayed edge, running embers) plus a few splashes, 22 billboarded (Y-only) quads with `fuego_lengua.gdshader` (procedural fbm flame, no textures): 1.2 m (`FUEGO_ALTO`) in the middle, 0.3 m at the edge. Sparks are a `CPUParticles3D` and the light flickers. The rag flame and the burst on impact (`ZonaLombrices.estallido`, replaces the `molotov_revienta` sheet) use the same shader (`ZonaLombrices.lengua`). The stain stretches toward the throw (`giro`, from the bottle's velocity) and the `Lanzador` aim marker is the same oval, rotated to match. The fixed tambo fire on the map is still pixel art.
- **Palomita** (JJ, 2026-11-10; the stun grenade): a folded-paper firework, `scripts/lombrices/Palomita.gd` (a `MeshInstance3D` that sets its shared `malla()` in `_init`, so `crear_modelo` can measure `scenes/palomita.tscn`): a magazine-paper triangle puffed in the middle with a yellow fuse, twice real size (`ANCHO` 0.26 m; JJ), wobble off (its 2.5 cm grid wrecked it). Item `resources/productos/palomita.tres` (`arrojar` 3). The `Lanzador`'s 3 now cycles salt → Molotov → palomita; the palomita charges and throws exactly like the Molotov (`_se_carga`, the same `OverhandThrow`), its floor ring scaled to `Arrojadizo.ATURDE_RADIO` and white (`COLOR_AREA_PALOMITA`; yellow once fully charged, like the Molotov's), and it shows in the right hand while held (`Lanzador._poner_en_mano`, `Palomita.EN_MANO`/`GIRO_MANO`: held by a base corner, sticking out away from the thumb, or it went into the thigh; recreated if `_mostrar_arma` freed it). It flies as `Arrojadizo` type `PALOMITA` (4; 3 is the Madre's spit) and on landing `_tronar` plays `disparo_pistola` at `TRUENO_TONO` 0.8 and the generated `zumbido_oidos` (a 3.9 kHz ear ring, 4 s, non-positional, `ZUMBIDO_CERCA`-`ZUMBIDO_LEJOS` dB by distance; sounds go on the parent because the node frees itself), flashes a light, and calls `Zombie.aturdir(length of the ring)` on every zombie within `ATURDE_RADIO` 7 m (HERIDO for that long, ignoring `ATURDIR`: `Hit_Head` for `TRUENO_DURA` 0.43 s, then `Crying`, hands over the face, both added to `animaciones.gd`; JJ picked them; the gordo has neither and sways in a slow idle; not the boss; `alertar` when it wears off) plus `Zombie.ruido` to `OYEN_TRUENO` 30 m. **Failures** (JJ, rolled in `Lanzador.soltar_molotov`): `CEBADA_PROB` 6 % it's a dud (`Arrojadizo.cebada`: lands, lies there `CEBADA_QUEDA` s with its fuse light off, a quiet `fizz`, and a rising fixed-size "¡Se cebó!" `Arrojadizo.letrero`); `TRUENA_MANO_PROB` 3 % it goes off in your hand at the release: `Arrojadizo.tronar` (static, returns the ring's length) at the hand stuns the zombies around you, "¡Te tronó en la mano!", and `Player.aturdirte(length, MANO_DANO 15)`: `_aturdido` s in `_ocupado`, no movement, `_unhandled_input` ignores everything but mouse look, the throw is cut (`_aventando` -1, or its end would drop the clip), `character_animation` entry `aturdido` (`Crying`, hands over the face) until `terminar_accion`, and the damage straight off `Salud` (not `recibir_dano`: it ignores invulnerability, rolling and blocking). The Madre and the crías are immune (JJ): `_tronar` only touches `Zombie.activos`. `tools/armas/palomita_foto.gd` renders the model and el_compa holding it (needs a window).
- **Bocina** (JJ, 2026-11-10; the decoy, like a monkey bomb but a Bluetooth speaker): models in code like the palomita, all on `ps1_combined` with wobble off: `Bocina` (a lying 26 cm cylinder of dark grille cloth, red rubber end caps with passive radiators, a top strip with three buttons and a blue LED; `Bocina.ondas()`), `BotellaAgua` (PET bottle with water, label and blue cap; the plastic is a transparent `StandardMaterial3D`) and `BocinaEncintada` (the speaker with the bottle beside it on the same floor and two grey tape bands around both, the convex hull of the two circles). Items `bocina.tres` (`arrojar` 4), `botella_agua.tres`, `bocina_encintada.tres` (`arrojar` 5); recipe `resources/recetas/bocina_encintada.tres` = bocina + botella de agua (two ingredients; the tape is only for looks, JJ). Not sold anywhere yet (the safehouse lays out every product). The `Lanzador` throws both like the Molotov (`_se_carga` is everything but salt; `ARROJADIZOS` maps item type to `Arrojadizo` type; its 3 now skips to the next type you carry), shows them in the hand (`_modelo_en_mano`, `Bocina.EN_MANO`: held in the middle like a can) and its white floor ring is the pull radius (`_radio_area`). It flies as `Arrojadizo` `BOCINA`/`BOCINA_AGUA` and on landing becomes a `scripts/map/Senuelo.gd` (static `activos`, group `senuelos`): it plays the generated `bocina_musica` (a 4 s loop at 120 bpm through a cheap-speaker chain: high-pass, tanh, clip, few bits; placeholder until JJ's own file goes in `Sonidos.ARCHIVOS`) on an `AudioStreamPlayer3D`, throbs to the beat, shows `Bocina.ondas()` (JJ picked two of four options: `shaders/ondas_bocina.gdshader`, white pixel rings growing on the floor with Bayer dither, which must write depth or the air shader's screen copy loses them, and `shaders/aire_bocina.gdshader`, the air rippling outward by reading `hint_screen_texture`, with a faint white edge; both swell on each beat), and every `AVISA_CADA` calls `Zombie.ruido`. `Zombie._destino` asks `Senuelo.la_que_oye` first: any zombie but the boss inside a speaker's `radio` walks straight to it (`_guia` null) ahead of you or the tinaco, and scratches it on arrival (`Senuelo.recibir_golpe` does nothing). Alone it barely works (`RADIO_SOLA` 6 m, `DURA_SOLA` 6 s; JJ); taped to the water bottle `RADIO_AGUA` 22 m, `DURA_AGUA` 10 s. Then it stops, plays `bocina_apaga`, shrinks and is freed, and the zombies go back to you. `tools/armas/bocina_foto.gd` (`--encintada`) renders the models, the waves and el_compa holding it.
- Items: `sal.tres` (tiendita), `alcohol.tres` (licorería; no recipe uses it any more), `diente_madre.tres` (trophy). **Molotov chain** (JJ, 2026-11-10): drinking a caguama (`caguama.tres`, `agua_mala.tres`) or a Jarritos leaves a `botella_vacia.tres` (one item whatever it came from): `InventoryItemData.deja` is what's left after Utilizar/Q, `Inventory.usar_curacion` puts it in the same slot, and an item with `deja` can be used with full life (the Jarritos now heals 25 like the caguama (JJ, 2026-11-10)). The gasolinera (below) fills an empty bottle: `gasolina limón` ($30) or `cereza` ($40, so limón is 25 % cheaper), `botella_gasolina_limon.tres`/`_cereza.tres`; those items carry `InventoryItemData.requiere` = `botella_vacia`, which `Interior._comprar` takes from your inventory (no empty bottle: "Te hace falta: botella vacía"; `Inventory.cambiar` puts the filled one in the same slot, so it doesn't need a free one). Filled bottle + `trapo.tres` (ferretería) = `molotov.tres` ("Molotov de limón", `arrojar` 2, `resources/recetas/molotov.tres`) or `molotov_cereza.tres` (`arrojar` 6, `resources/recetas/molotov_cereza.tres`). **The cherry one burns 1.5× as far** (`Arrojadizo.CEREZA_ALCANCE`, `fuego_radio(tipo)`: 3 m against the lemon's `FUEGO_RADIO` 2 m; same duration): it flies as `Arrojadizo.MOLOTOV_CEREZA` (7), `Lanzador.ARROJADIZOS[6]`, its floor ring and its `molotov_revienta` sheet scale with it, and the libreta's "remedios" data lists both. The Madre's arena only hands out the lemon one (`PruebaLombrices.MOLOTOV` = `molotov.tres`; JJ: the one used there is lemon). The bottles are one model in code, `scripts/lombrices/BotellaMolotov.gd` (JJ's reference: a liquor bottle with a rag): lathed transparent glass (an unshaded `StandardMaterial3D`, since a lit one washed the contents white), an old unbranded label and red neck seal (`ps1_combined`, wobble and jitter off), in three `etapa`s: `VACIA`, `GASOLINA` (liquid by `sabor`: green `LIMON`, red `CEREZA`) and `MOLOTOV` (plus a thick rag stuffed in the mouth, bent over and hanging down one side). `scenes/botella_vacia.tscn`, `botella_gasolina_limon.tscn`, `botella_gasolina_cereza.tscn`, `molotov.tscn` and `molotov_cereza.tscn` set them (the setters swap the mesh, so `crear_modelo` measures the right one). It's also the Molotov in the hand (`Lanzador._modelo_en_mano`, `EN_MANO`/`GIRO_MANO`: across the palm, neck past the thumb) and in flight, where the rag burns (JJ): the tambos' `fuego_tambo` pixel loop at `TRAPO_FUEGO` 0.06 on `BotellaMolotov.MECHA` plus a flickering light (`MECHA_LUZ`). `tools/armas/molotov_foto.gd` renders the five bottles, el_compa holding one and the flight at night (needs a window). The rag item is `models/objetos/trapo`.
- **Gasolinera** (JJ, 2026-11-10): the map uses **Lowpoly Gas Station** by AspectStudios (`models/tiendas/gasolinera/`, CC BY, downloaded with `fetch_sketchfab.py tiendas/gasolinera` and baked to `gasolinera.scn`; the Pemex model stays on disk unused; 14 m of frontage like before, `prefab_rotacion` -90: its +X, with the pumps' island and the price sign, faces the street): a white shop with double doors, a canopy on one tall white column, an island with two pumps, a price sign. JJ's colouring: the red bars (roof trim of the shop and canopy, the island and the pumps' hoods) are **green** and the blue nozzle is **red** (next to the green one: lemon and cherry). The model paints everything from one 8×8 flat-colour palette (`textures/Palette_baseColor.png`) that also holds the shop's merchandise, so `textures/Palette_verde.png` (palette cells `be1e2d` → green, `1e1eb4` → red, made with a short PIL script) is only assigned to the exterior meshes `Gas_Pumps_Palette_0` and `Store_Palette_0` (`MapPuntos._pintar_gasolinera`, from `_instanciar`). **Textures** (JJ: it looked like a cartoon): every mesh that uses the palette gets `shaders/ps1_gasolinera.gdshader` (`MapPuntos._texturar_gasolinera`, two shared materials: the original palette and the green one), which turns each palette colour into a material: white paint → stucco grain (`textures/map/gasolinera_aplanado.png`) with big concrete blotches, greys (the posts and columns) → pitted concrete with a hairline crack (`gasolinera_concreto.png`), green/red → grainy painted iron, the blue glass → dark with a diagonal glint. Both textures are made by `python3 -I tools/make_texturas_gasolinera.py` (tileable, 128 px) and projected triplanar in model space (so they follow the walls whatever the lot's rotation), with `gastado()` on top (grime rising from the floor, drips, dust, scratches, chipped paint on the painted iron) and the zone's `deterioro`; no vertex jitter (`PS1_MUNDO`). It used to have one box collider for the whole model, so you couldn't reach the pumps. `MapPuntos._solidos_gasolinera` now only makes solid the boxes of `GASOLINERA_SOLIDOS` (measured on the fused meshes, in model space: the island step, each pump with its posts, the canopy column, the shop, the cardboard boxes; `_caja_solida`: floor to top), so you can walk in from the street and beside the pumps (checked with a capsule: street to both arrows; you can't climb the 50 cm island). The model's y = 0 is its ground (`GASOLINERA_SUELO_Y`: the shop's foundation sits 20 cm lower and the prefab rests on the lowest point, which left the pumps floating). It also sets the holder's metas `surtidor`, `despachador` and `puerta_tienda` (`GASOLINERA_FLECHA`, `GASOLINERA_DESPACHADOR`, `GASOLINERA_PUERTA_TIENDA`, model space), and `_make_punto` puts **two yellow arrows** instead of one at the street edge (`_marcadores_gasolinera`): in front of the island gap beside the right-hand pump with the despachador, a random civilian (`MapPuntos.civil`, the same one that serves you inside; `_vendedor(punto, atras)` stands him on the island next to the pump, facing the street), giro `gasolinera`; and in front of the shop's double doors, giro `oxxo` (`GASOLINERA_TIENDA_GIRO`: the old `tiendita.tscn`, which no longer sells gasoline). `_mostrar_flecha` hides both at night. `Interiores.escenas["gasolinera"]` is `scenes/interiores/gasolinera.tscn` in `map_test`, the safehouse and `locales_preview`: `scripts/interiores/Gasolinera.gd` (`@tool`, extends `Interior`, overrides `_reconstruir`): a change of angle (camera low and close, fov 42) on the island under the canopy, open at the sides: asphalt, the raised island with Pemex bollards, a pump (green and white, green and red panels on its head, a green hose and a red hose with their nozzles, one on each side), a second island with two pumps behind, columns, the dark canopy with two light strips, the shop at the back, extra lights; the `Vendedor` (despachador) stands left of the pump, and the `Vitrina` shows the two bottles (limón green, cereza red) with `TiendaUI`'s price strip. `precio_factor` 1.0 here (the old tiendita's ×1.15 made the prices uneven).
