## Real-world map pipeline (`tools/map_import/`)

`models/zona_mapa.glb` is a real area of Zapopan built from OpenStreetMap with OSM2World 0.4.0 (Java). It is gitignored (~47 MB): the game only loads the chunks in `resources/map/cuadros/`, which are committed. Regenerate the .glb with the commands below before running `split_cuadros.gd`. The bbox lives in `tools/map_import/area.env`. `README.md` has the user-facing (Spanish) project overview and `docs/mapa/README.md` the full map guide (keep it in sync when the map changes; `docs/.gdignore` keeps its screenshots out of the Godot import).

```bash
bash tools/map_import/fetch_osm.sh      # Overpass -> data/map/area.osm (needs a User-Agent; falls back to mirrors on 504)
python3 -I tools/map_import/fetch_denue.py  # INEGI DENUE -> data/map/denue_puntos.json (stores/puestos)
python3 -I tools/map_import/fetch_google_buildings.py  # streams Google Open Buildings tile 843 (~1.7 GB, ~3 min), keeps only the bbox -> data/map/google_buildings.csv
tools/map_import/bin/venv/bin/python -I tools/map_import/fetch_satelite.py  # tree crowns + grass score -> data/map/satelite_* (venv: rasterio numpy scipy)
bash tools/map_import/build_glb.sh      # downloads OSM2World to tools/map_import/bin/ (gitignored, ~450 MB), runs prepare_osm.py, writes the .glb
godot --path . --headless --import      # runs scripts/map/MapPostImport.gd on the .glb
godot --path . --headless -s tools/map_import/split_cuadros.gd  # one .scn per chunk -> resources/map/cuadros/ (what the game loads)
godot --path . scenes/map_test.tscn     # walk the map
```

- `prepare_osm.py` crops ways to the bbox (+30 m), gives buildings without height 1–2 levels, a flat roof and a facade color (deterministic per OSM id), applies `data/map/overrides.json` (`"way/<id>": {tag: value|null}` — manual fixes from looking at Street View), and writes `<bounds>` so the model origin is the bbox center.
- `tools/map_import/osm2world.properties` is appended to OSM2World's `standard.properties`; OSM2World must run from its own folder because texture paths are relative.
- `build_glb.sh` runs OSM2World with `--lod 2`: below 2 OSM2World drops sidewalks and kerbs. LOD 2 would add 3D windows (GLASS/PLASTIC meshes); `prepare_osm.py` tags every building `window=no`, which keeps the LOD 1 materials (`Plaster002`/`Concrete034`). Once, it downsizes OSM2World's own textures to 256 px with ImageMagick and strips their ICC profiles (a gray ICC on 3-channel JPGs, e.g. Plastic010, made Java abort the whole export). Result: ~22 MB. `tools/map_import/bin/.gdignore` keeps Godot from importing OSM2World's bundled models/textures. OSM2World skips meshes that fail instead of exiting with an error, so the script checks `bin/osm2world.log` and fails if the .glb has no `Building` nodes.
- `MapPostImport.gd` (set as `import_script/path` in `zona_mapa.glb.import`) swaps every material for a `ps1_combined` ShaderMaterial (tint = original albedo color, `use_vertex_color = true` because OSM2World stores each house's `building:colour` as vertex color), adds trimesh collision except to vegetation, and sets `visibility_range_end`. The import keeps textures embedded (`gltf/embedded_image_handling=3`).
- Editing `MapPostImport.gd` does **not** trigger a reimport, and neither does `touch`: delete `.godot/imported/zona_mapa.glb-*` and run `--import` again. If an import fails, Godot writes `valid=false` into `zona_mapa.glb.import` and never retries; delete that line.
- OSM only has ~970 buildings here (one housing development north of the center); Microsoft footprints have even fewer (~140). `prepare_osm.py` fills the gaps with Google Open Buildings (CC BY 4.0): it keeps confidence ≥ 0.70 and area ≥ 20 m², skips any footprint that overlaps an OSM building, and adds the rest as ways with ids from `2*10**15` and `source=Google Open Buildings v3`. Result: ~7,900 buildings.
- The post-import merges the ~18k OSM2World meshes into 128 m chunks (one ArrayMesh per material per chunk, vegetation in a separate chunk without collision): ~200 nodes, ~85 draw calls. Per-building nodes and OSM ids don't survive the import.
- **Streaming**: `map_test.tscn` does not instance `zona_mapa.glb`. `split_cuadros.gd` saves each chunk as `resources/map/cuadros/cuadro_<x>_<z>[_vegetacion].scn`, shares the 15 materials as `materiales/*.res`, and writes `indice.json` with each chunk's real XZ extent. A mesh is assigned to the chunk of its center, so long roads and power lines make some chunks up to ~1.2 km wide. `scripts/map/MapStreamer.gd` (`Mapa` node, `target_path` = Player) loads synchronously the chunks within `load_radius` (300 m, measured to the extent rectangle) at start, then loads the rest with `ResourceLoader.load_threaded_request`, and frees the ones beyond `unload_radius` (400 m). **On foot** those radii are capped by `CargaCuadro.carga()`/`descarga()` at `CARGA_A_PIE` 200 / `DESCARGA_A_PIE` 260 m, in `MapStreamer`, `MapCasas`, `MapArboles`, `MapBardas` and `MapBodegas`, and at 150 / 200 m in `MapPuntos`, because fog hides nearly everything past that; `MapDron` sets `CargaCuadro.volando` and the drone gets the full radii. `MapAlumbrado` keeps its long radius so far lamps still show at night. About 16 of the 200 chunks are loaded at a time on foot (~31 flying). **Measuring**: `godot --path . tools/medir_mapa.tscn` opens a window for 2-3 min and prints FPS, frame times, draw calls and primitives at fixed spots, day and night (`--rapido`, `--far=`, `--capturas=`, `--apagar=Nodo,Nodo`, `--poner=Nodo.prop=valor`). Results on 2026-10-31, before → after the caps and shorter interior range: spawn by day 234 → 262 FPS, Parques del Bosque by day 303 → 349, walk by day 206 → 239 with the longest frame 21.5 → 12.2 ms. Stores were the largest single cost (spawn without `Puntos`: 367 FPS). The refuge costs nothing measurable. Capping the player camera `far` at 180 m gave only 3-10 % and risks far lamps, so it isn't applied. `medir_mapa` ends with a full 30-zombie horde: 102-107 FPS, longest frame 15.6 ms. With people on the streets by day: spawn ~249 FPS, Parques ~304. **No vertex snapping on the world**: see `PS1_MUNDO` in `shaders/ps1_vertex.gdshaderinc` and `MapStreamer._quitar_brinco`; measured as the worst camera step over the typical one: store 1.4 → 1.1, wall 1.3 → 1.1. Graffiti atlas cells went from 128 to 256 px (`Calcomanias.DISENO_PX`). **Per-frame load budget**: `scripts/map/CargaCuadro.gd` (preloaded, static `hay()`/`gastar(inicio)`, `PRESUPUESTO_US` 3 ms shared per frame, the first caller always gets one piece) throttles MapStreamer (`_add` of finished chunks), MapCasas/MapArboles/MapAlumbrado (`_por_crear` queue, nearest cell first, built in `_crear_pendientes`), MapPuntos (same, per punto) and MapPasto. They used to build every new cell in the frame that checked distance, and all of them checked in the same frame: flying at 45 m/s gave 120–170 ms frames (now ≤ ~60 ms, profiled with the drone). `_ready` still builds what's in range synchronously (`_crear_pendientes(true)`). New streaming systems should use it too. `MapPuntos` streams stores the same way at runtime (150/200 m on foot, 250/320 m flying) but shows all of them in the editor. The chunks must be regenerated after every reimport of the .glb. In the editor, `map_test.tscn` shows no map, because MapStreamer is not `@tool`.
- **Drone mode**: the `Dron` node (`scripts/map/MapDron.gd`) in `map_test.tscn` starts active (`iniciar_en_dron`). It flies at a fixed `altura` (30 m) at `velocidad` (15 m/s, Shift ×3) with the camera pitched down; WASD moves it, and mouse or Q/E rotates it. Fog drops to `niebla_dron` while flying. **V** switches to the player, who is frozen while flying and lands under the drone via a downward raycast (street or roof). `MapStreamer` and `MapPuntos` use `Dron` as `target_path`; when not flying, the drone follows the player.
- The OSM2World textures are downsized to 256 px and forced to PNG32 or color JPG; gray+alpha PNGs make Java fail with `Unknown image type 0` and OSM2World silently drops every mesh using the texture atlas.
- **Facades**: `MapPostImport.BUILDING_ROLES` maps the only two building materials at LOD 1 (`Plaster002` walls, `Concrete034` flat roofs; Concrete034 is also used by sidewalks, so it only applies to meshes under a `Building*` node) to `shaders/ps1_facade.gdshader`. That shader picks a random tile per 2.5 m bay and level from `textures/map/fachadas.png` (row 1 = ground floor: door/cortina/window/zaguán; rows 2–3 deteriorated) and tints the wall part (`fachadas_mascara.png`) with the vertex color. The textures come from `python3 -I tools/map_import/make_textures.py`, either `--estilo dibujado` (default, all hand-drawn) or `--estilo fotos` (wall plaster, door, shutters and roofs cropped from CC0 Poly Haven photos downloaded to `tools/map_import/bin/texturas_cc0/`; windows are still drawn). Chunk materials reference these PNGs by path, so after regenerating them a plain `--import` is enough. Rows 2–3 of `fachadas.png` are the deteriorated variants (drawn in both styles; `draw_args` also takes `(color, [(x, y), ...])` lines/polygons). The current textures use `--estilo fotos`. The same script writes `grietas.png`, `suelo.png` (dark green base grass), `banqueta.png` (sidewalk), `pasto*.png`, `asfalto.png`, `empedrado.png`, `mata_pasto.png` and `arbol.png`. `MapPostImport.GROUND_TEXTURES` draws OSM2World's `TERRAIN_DEFAULT` and non-building `Concrete034` with `shaders/ps1_suelo.gdshader` and those textures (world-space tiling every `tile_size` m, mipmapped, jitter/wobble off); changing that dict needs a reimport plus `split_cuadros.gd`. The `Suelo` plane in `map_test.tscn` (5 cm under the map) uses the same shader and texture: where they overlap the image is identical, so z-fighting doesn't show. Photo textures through `ps1_combined` (`filter_nearest`, no mipmaps) shimmer badly on the ground. The other flat ground materials (`MapPostImport.FLAT_GROUND`: asphalt, grass, water...) keep `ps1_combined` with jitter/wobble off: wobble moves vertices up to 2.5 cm in depth, so distant roads sank under the non-wobbling dirt/sidewalk and flickered. The PS1 vertex stage is shared through `shaders/ps1_vertex.gdshaderinc`, which `ps1_combined` also includes.
- **Stores (puntos)**: `python3 -I tools/map_import/fetch_denue.py` downloads INEGI's DENUE for Jalisco (~40 MB zip, Latin-1 CSV), keeps the bbox and maps SCIAN codes to tipos (`TIPOS_SCIAN`; OXXO and purificadoras by name; ferreteria 467111, purificadora 312112; licoreria 461211/461212 or a `tienda` whose name matches `LICORERIA` (depósito, Six, Modelorama, vinos, cerveza...); `taqueria` 722513/722514 only if the name matches `TACOS` (taco, taquer, carnitas, barbacoa, birri, pastor, vapor: tortas ahogadas, cenadurías, menuderías stay houses, at the user's request); semifijo = `puesto_tacos` if food (`GIROS_COMIDA`) with a taco name, `puesto` (tejuino cart) if other food or 4611xx, else dropped (llantera, ropa, celulares); tortillerías are gone) -> `data/map/denue_puntos.json`. Google Maps/Places can't be used: their terms forbid extracting places. `data/map/puntos.json` is for manual points plus an `omitir` list of `origen` ids to drop bad DENUE/OSM points; `prepare_osm.py` also takes shop/amenity points from OSM. First `cambiar_tipo` (`data/map/puntos.json` {origen: tipo}) and `subtipo` set the drawing tipo (`tienda` → `licoreria` or `abarrotes`; every `farmacia*` → `farmacia`; OSM `shop=alcohol` → licoreria; `TIPOS_QUITADOS` drops tortillerías), then it keeps only one point per family within 40 m (`FAMILIA`: `puesto_tacos` counts as `taqueria`, and the local wins over the puesto; 80 m for `abarrotes`, 15 m for `farmacia` so every pharmacy stays and only the DENUE/OSM copy of the same one merges, `DUPLICATE_DIST_TIPO`; the user found 80/150 m left too few stores) (priority local > puesto, then manual > DENUE > OSM; ~165 points), snaps each store to the house it's in or the nearest one within 15 m (houses > 300 m² are skipped: Google merges whole rows), prefers houses on the point's side of the street (any side if the point is in the carriageway), recomputes the facing from the house it snapped to (prefab front = +Z) and removes that house; `puesto*` don't remove houses and always go on the sidewalk, front `PUESTO_GUARNICION` behind the kerb. Then `alejar_de_calles` pushes every store/puesto (real footprint from the huellas) out of the carriageway + sidewalk of every `BLOCK_STREET_TYPES` street (service is skipped: gas-station/parking driveways; a puesto ignores its own street), so DENUE points in the middle of a street end with the facade on the nearest sidewalk edge. Names go through `parodia` (`MARCAS_PARODIA`: OXO, Dr Simio, Guadalaraja, Perez; only text, the models still show the real brand). Tipos in `LOCALES_HILERA` are moved by `place_casas(locales=)` into the model-house row of their block face (facade on the face's median `setback`, frontage `LOCAL_ANCHO` 4.5–9 m, depth `LOCAL_FONDO`; if the lot hits another store footprint or lot it tries up to `LOCAL_INTENTOS` positions beside what's in the way; if none fits it stays where `place_puntos` put it only if nothing is there, else it's dropped (`_quitar`) — the old code left it in place on top of its neighbour, which is how stores overlapped); their interval joins the run and is subtracted exactly, so `llenar_tramo` puts houses flush against them. `pisos_locales` gives every local the `pisos` of the nearest model house (`LOCAL_PISOS_VECINA` 25 m; else mostly 1). `quitar_negocios_encimados` (right before writing) drops any business whose footprint still overlaps another (puesto loses to local/model, then `INTERACTIVO_PRIORIDAD`, then origin). `elegir_puntos` marks one 1-floor model house between two neighbours per colonia as `punto` (illegal sales) and adds it as tipo `punto`. `marcar_interactivos` (after the houses) writes `giro` (`GIROS`), `aire_libre` (`GIRO_AIRE_LIBRE`) and `interactivo`: one per giro within `INTERACTIVO_DIST` 80 m (abarrotes 150 m, punto unlimited; oxxo, farmacia, gasolinera first, `INTERACTIVO_PRIORIDAD`); `licoreria` is its own giro (`licoreria.tscn`). The result goes to `resources/map/puntos_mapa.json` (written after the houses) with local x/z, `rotacion_y`, and the lot's `ancho`/`fondo`. `MapPuntos` puts a semi-transparent yellow rotating arrow (static `MapPuntos.flecha()`; only drawn between `MARCADOR_CERCA` 2 m and `MARCADOR_VISTA` 18 m from the camera, self-fading) + `Area3D` (`_marcador`, `prefab_puerta` lateral offset) in front of every `interactivo`; stepping on it with the `Player` emits `entrar_negocio(tipo, giro, nombre, aire_libre, marcador)` (`marcador` = the Area3D, +Z toward the street), which `Interiores` handles (see **Interiors**); without a listener, or for giros without a scene or vendor, it shows "<nombre>: Próximamente" (`MapPuntos.avisar`). Interactive `aire_libre` points whose tipo is in `vendedor_atras` (puesto 2.6 m = behind the cart) or that set `punto["vendedor_atras"]` (the puesto de tacos, `_make_puesto_tacos`) get a `Vendedor` (`vendedor_escena` = `npc.tscn`, its TalkArea turned off so the jump button doesn't talk to it) as a child of the marker, that far behind the arrow facing the street, with a line per giro (`VENDEDOR_DIALOGO`). The punto has no street vendor: it has an interior scene. `punto` only gets the arrow (MapCasas draws the house). The minimap draws only `interactivo` stores and never the punto. `scripts/map/MapPuntos.gd` (`Puntos` node in `map_test.tscn`) builds the casas-local and puestos de tacos (see **Store models**) or instantiates `prefabs[tipo]`, or a colored placeholder box, at runtime; its children are not saved to the scene. Changing puntos requires rerunning `build_glb.sh` and reimporting, because the replaced houses disappear from the .glb.
- **Sketchfab models**: `SKETCHFAB_TOKEN=... python3 -I tools/map_import/fetch_sketchfab.py` downloads the models listed in `MODELS` (key = folder under `models/`: `tiendas/`, `casas/`, `props/`, `objetos/`) as `scene.gltf`, shrinks their textures to 256 px, writes a `.import` with `scripts/map/TiendaPostImport.gd` and appends the credits to `CREDITS.md` (one section per top folder). `TiendaPostImport` swaps materials for `ps1_combined` (albedo only), can crop scenes listed in `RECORTES` (currently none) and reduces models in `MAX_TRIANGULOS` with `ImporterMesh.generate_lods` (meshoptimizer; photogrammetry scans full of UV seams stop at ~1/8). Blender decimation and `SurfaceTool.generate_lod` were tried and wreck scanned models. `fetch_sketchfab.py` only shrinks textures of fresh downloads (mogrify rewrites files even when the size doesn't change). Removed at the user's request after testing (scans looked broken or were too heavy): House Porch Toluca, Mexican Auto Repair Shop (taller), Carrito de Tamales and the "Tacos" stand. Five requested models can't be downloaded through the API and were dropped: Street buildings, Taco – Mexican Food (paid), Telmex Payphone, Mexican Construction Worker, Chivas.
- **Only baked models are in git**: the game loads `models/<grupo>/<modelo>/<modelo>.scn`, written by `godot --path . --headless -s tools/map_import/hornear_modelos.gd` from the imported `scene.gltf` (already reduced and with PS1 materials; LODs stripped, compressed, UID kept across rebakes). `scene.gltf`, `scene.bin`, their `.import` and the unused texture maps (normal, metallicRoughness, emissive, occlusion, specular) are gitignored; only `textures/*_baseColor`/`_diffuse`, `LICENSE_sketchfab.txt` and `preview.png` are committed next to the `.scn`. To change a model's reduction, tints or materials: re-download with `fetch_sketchfab.py` (it re-downloads any folder without `scene.gltf`), `--import`, then rerun `hornear_modelos.gd` (and `separar_pack.gd`/`separar_simi.gd` if it's one of those). `map_test.tscn` references the `.scn` files. The originals are still in git history (commits before 2026-10-27).
- **Game assets (not placed yet)**: `fetch_sketchfab.py` also downloads `models/personajes/` (NPCs, zombies, Simi), `models/armas/`, `models/interiores/` and inventory/mission objects in `models/objetos/` (list and status in `docs/especificaciones.md` §10). `TiendaPostImport.TINTES` repaints materials by name (tejuino, trapo); `GRUPOS_PS1_EQUIPO` (personajes, armas, objetos, interiores) use the team's values `snap_resolution` 80 and `wobble_resolution` 80; affine stays off everywhere because Sketchfab texture atlases smear with the affine warp (tested even at `warp_strength` 8). `SIN_WOBBLE` turns wobble off for tiny objects (the 2.5 cm view-space snap collapsed the 1 cm pill into a triangle). The PS1 material replaces the original inside the mesh (not as a surface override), so baked `.scn` files don't reference the gitignored normal/roughness maps. "Comida Low Poly" (`models/interiores/comida`, CC BY, 121 loose ~50-triangle pieces with generic names) is split by `godot --path . --headless -s tools/map_import/separar_comida.gd` into `models/interiores/comida/piezas/<categoria>_NN.res` meshes (metres, 0.13 m per model unit, base at y = 0, label facing +Z; `CATEGORIAS` maps tree indices found with a contact sheet: charola, bote, lacteo, papas, cereal, galletas, aceite, refresco, limpieza, pan, vino, fruta); it isn't baked (`SOLO_PIEZAS`). `godot --path . --headless -s tools/map_import/separar_mercancia.gd` does the same for other interiors into `models/interiores/<carpeta>/piezas/` (`tiendita`: `cigarros` = the store's cigarette pack in 6 `tintes`, `dulce` = gansito, takis, mazapán; `ferreteria`, the default): tools from three Sketchfab packs lying flat with each tool in several meshes (`interiores/herramientas`, `herramientas_2`, `herramientas_psx`; meshes whose AABBs touch form one tool, stood up with the face to +Z, per-pack `escala`) → `herramienta`, the PSX machete and `models/armas/machete` → `machete`, `models/armas/bate` → `bate`, `models/objetos/cinta` → `cinta`, `interiores/bote_pintura(_2)` → `pintura`, `interiores/aerosol(_2)` → `aerosol` (whole models scaled to `alto`); those packs are in `SOLO_PIEZAS` too. `fetch_sketchfab.py <carpeta>...` only processes those folders (without arguments it re-downloads every model whose `scene.gltf` is missing). Only `mochila` and `mazapan` are split from the Mexican Starter Pack; the pack itself isn't baked (`hornear_modelos.SOLO_PIEZAS`) and its other textures are gitignored. Sketchfab only offers the Mexican Starter Pack as one download: `godot --path . --headless -s tools/map_import/separar_pack.gd` saves each piece as `models/objetos/mexican_starter_pack/<pieza>.scn`; `separar_simi.gd` saves the dancing Simi costume (Mixamo rig, `bailar` loop) from the Similares kiosk as `models/personajes/dr_simi/simi_botarga.scn`. Rerun both after reimporting those models. `python3 -I tools/fetch_cc0.py` downloads the CC0 Quaternius Universal Animation Library 1+2 to `models/animaciones/` (own humanoid rig, not Mixamo: use Godot retargeting). `godot --path . -s tools/map_import/capturas.gd [-- --todas]` (needs a window) writes `preview.png` into each model folder (`<name>_preview.png` next to loose `.scn`); skinned models are framed by their bones because their mesh AABB ignores the pose. `fetch_sketchfab.py` waits and retries on HTTP 429.
- **Gasolinera** (JJ, 2026-11-10): the map uses the Lowpoly Gas Station model (`models/tiendas/gasolinera/`, its red bars painted green and its blue nozzle red through `Palette_verde.png`, only on the exterior meshes); it has no whole-model box any more: `MapPuntos._solidos_gasolinera` makes solid only the boxes of `GASOLINERA_SOLIDOS` so you can walk in, and puts two arrows (the pump's, with a random-civilian despachador, into `scenes/interiores/gasolinera.tscn`; the shop's, into the OXXO-style `tiendita.tscn`). Details in `CLAUDE.md` ("Gasolinera").
- **Store models**: only the gasolinera (Lowpoly Gas Station, AspectStudios), the `puesto` (Low Poly Street Food Cart, a tejuino cart) and the `alto` (Señal Alto) are Sketchfab models, assigned in `map_test.tscn` → `Puntos.prefabs` with `prefab_rotacion` in degrees so the front faces +Z; `MapPuntos` scales each uniformly to `prefab_ancho[tipo]` metres of frontage, puts the facade on the lot's street edge and lets it grow backwards and sideways; `prepare_osm.quitar_edificios_encimados` then removes every OSM/Google building, and every model-house lot, that overlaps a store's real footprint (inset 0.3 m). Footprints come from `data/map/huellas_tiendas.json`, written by `tools/map_import/huellas_tiendas.gd` (run by `build_glb.sh`, output silenced, falls back to the old json if Godot fails; it calls `MapPuntos.huella_modelo` for `prefabs`, writes `_locales` = `GIROS_LOCAL` keys + purificadora, which `huella_negocio` checks before `modelos`, and `casas`: native frontage/depth of each `Casas.modelos` via `MapCasas._preparar_modelo`). The Tienda 3B, Farmacia Guadalajara, OXXO, Similares kiosk and Cara1 taco stand were dropped at the user's request (they didn't line up with the houses) and stay on disk unused, like `oxxo_abandonado`, `oxxo_letrero`, `oxxo_mexican`, `barrio_mexicano`, `tacos_props`, `casas/multifamiliar` and `models/objetos/*`. **Casas-local** (`MapPuntos.GIROS_LOCAL`: abarrotes white/red, licoreria Corona blue/yellow, farmacia white/blue with a flag cross and the dancing `simi_botarga.scn` (`botarga` export) on the sidewalk, ferreteria Truper orange/black, oxxo white with a red `banda`, taqueria white/red) are built from boxes by `_make_local_giro` (`_medidas`: lot-sized, min `LOCAL_MIN`; `PLANTA_BAJA` 3.2 + `PISO_ALTO` 3.0 per extra floor + `PRETIL`, so 1 or 2 floors match the Urbi houses; side pillars, a room `LOCAL_CUARTO` deep up to `LOCAL_SALA_MAX` wide, a door `LOCAL_PUERTA_FRACCION` of it with a `_marco` (jambs and head, no sill) and the cortina rolled up inside; upper floor with barred windows); no text anywhere (the user's request; `letrero` is only the name shown on entering if the business has none). Through the door you see the giro's interior scene (`Interiores.escenas`, read through `interiores_path`): `_interior_local` instantiates it, keeps `Mostrador`, `Vendedor` (TalkArea off), `Muebles`, `Refrigeradores`, `Letrero`, scales it to the room, adds wall linings with the scene's guardapolvo and its lights (shadowless; the counter light only if `luz_mostrador`), and `_relleno` fills the empty side walls with `RELLENO` pieces (fridges, shelves, chip racks) only for abarrotes, licoreria and farmacia. Space (group `interactuable`) opens `scripts/map/TragamonedasUI.gd`, loaded with `load()`: it uses the `Cartera` autoload, and `tools/` scripts run with `-s` load `MapPuntos` before autoloads exist, so a direct class reference breaks `huellas_tiendas.gd`. Bets come from `Cartera.pesos` ($1 each, max 9 per fruit) and unspun bets return on leaving. A win = prize above the total bet (a coin on every fruit always "hits" while losing money). `Cohetes.lanzar` adds the fireworks to the scene root; its material has `disable_fog` (the map's fog hid them) and on prizes ≥ `PREMIO_CAMARA` the camera pulls back at launch. Sounds are generated in code (`Maquinita.sonido`); the repo has no audio files. **Co-op**: no networking exists; everything others must see goes through `Maquinita.mostrar_*`, `elegir_boton` and `apretar_boton`, which decide nothing and would become `@rpc("call_local")` with the server running `Tragamonedas`. Ideas for NPC players and weekday difficulty: `docs/tragamonedas_notas.md`.
- `data/.gdignore` exists because Godot would otherwise import `google_buildings.csv` as a translation file.
- Map data is ODbL; keep the attribution in `CREDITS.md`. Don't use Google Maps or Street View data for geometry: their terms forbid it, so Street View is only a visual reference for writing overrides.


## Map in Blender (`tools/blender/mapa/`, `scenes/mapa.tscn`)

The hand-made replacement for the OSM map, built one colonia at a time (plan agreed with AG on 2026-11-10; design docs in `docs/mapa/colonias/`, pilot Parques de Santa Mugre). `map_test.tscn` stays as it is until the new map can replace it.

- **Source of truth is each colonia's JSON** (`tools/blender/mapa/colonias/<colonia>.json`): the Blender scene is rebuilt from it by `colonia.py`, and `leer()` writes hand moves in Blender back into it (objects carry a `dato` custom property: `pieza:<name>` or `marca:<name>`). `exportar.py` calls `leer()` first, so the JSON and the .glb never disagree. Claude edits it through the Blender MCP.
- **Coordinates**: world metres in the real map frame (`resources/map/minimapa.json`), so each crop sits at its real position and `ZonasMapa`, `MapMinimapa`, `MapaUI` and the map pieces keep working. Godot (x, y, z) = Blender (x, -z, y); Godot yaw = Blender Z rotation (a piece's front, Godot +Z, is Blender -Y). `referencia.py` uses `prepare_osm.py`'s `local_xz` projection (origin at the centre of `area.env`; `area.osm` has no `<bounds>`).
- **Output**: `models/mapa/<colonia>/<colonia>.glb` (only the `Mundo` collection: geometry plus `-colonly` boxes, which Godot turns into StaticBody3D on layer 1, so zombies, `CampoFlujo` and walking checks work unchanged), its `.blend` in `fuente/` behind a `.gdignore`, `resources/mapa/<colonia>/marcas.json` and `puntos.json` (the `negocios` list, in `MapPuntos`' schema, for the minimap, `Gente`, missions and `Partida`).
- **`scripts/mapa/Colonia.gd`** instances the .glb and builds gameplay from the markers: `spawn` moves the player in `Colonia._enter_tree` (the node sits before the player, the drone and, later, `Supervivencia`: `MapDron` reads the player's position in its `_enter_tree` and lands the player under itself in `_ready`, which undid a spawn set in `_ready`), `yonke` builds `YonkeMapa` with its own barda, `puerta` builds the yellow arrow `Area3D` (the `YonkeMapa` pattern) that emits `MapPuntos.entrar_negocio(giro, giro, texto, false, area)`, with meta `civil` from its name. Its +Z faces the street (`Interiores._salir` leaves the player 2.2 m along it). Unknown marker types warn.
- **`scenes/mapa.tscn`**: the `map_test` node set without the OSM builders (`Mapa` streamer, `Casas`, `Bodegas`, `Bardas`, `Pasto`). Buildings are modelled in the .glb, so the `Puntos` node reads the colonia's `resources/mapa/santa_mugre/puntos.json` (written by `exportar.py`: the `negocios` plus the hand-placed `puestos_calle`) and builds each business with the same code as `map_test`: the casa-local facade painted in the giro's colours with its shutter rolled up and the interior seen through the door, the seller, the sidewalk stall of the taquerías, the fruit machines and the loose taco and tejuino stands. A negocio marked `ajustado` (all the ones the Blender map places) uses its house's footprint as is (`MapPuntos._medidas`, no `LOCAL_MIN` growing it) and the heights of the neighbouring houses (`PLANTA_AJUSTADA`/`PISO_AJUSTADO`/`PRETIL_AJUSTADO`, the kit's `PISO` and `PRETIL`). `bloqueo._tiendas` gives each negocio the nearest free house that is at least `LOCAL_MIN` (4.5 × 6 m) and at most `LOCAL_MAX` (14 × 16 m) within `LOCAL_RADIO` 25 m and whose front faces a real street (`_da_a_calle`); that house gets `local: true`, `colonia.py` skips it (the facade is `MapPuntos`'s), its `puerta` mark gets `local` so `Colonia.gd` doesn't draw a second arrow and sign, and El Punto (`sin_flecha`) keeps its Blender house and door. `python bloqueo.py` with `REASIGNAR = True` (see `reasignar_negocios`) redoes only this choice without reseeding the rest of the map. The trees (`ficus` pieces) are the two trees of *Tacos_ Props* (Elbolillo, CC BY 4.0): `extraer_arbol_tacos.gd` writes `tools/blender/mapa/modelos/arbol_tacos.json` and `textures/mapa/arbol_{corteza,hojas}.png`, `Constructor.arbol_modelo` builds them and `MapaPostImport` draws `arbol_hojas` with the alpha-cutout mural shader. For now it has only the environment, `Ciclo`, `Colonia`, the player, drone, minimap, `Interiores` and `InventoryUI`; zombies, `Supervivencia`, `Partida`, `Gente`, lamps and trees come with the gameplay markers (step 5, which also turns `Partida`'s `*_JSON` constants into exports).
- **Shutters**: modelled stores don't get `MapPuntos`' `Cortina` or the night closing visuals; that has to come back as a kit piece (pending).
- **Block-out** (`bloqueo.py`): seeds the colonia JSON from `referencia.datos_osm` with pieces marked `"auto": true` (rerunning replaces only those): OSM streets clipped to the crop (`calle`; one-way avenue carriageways get `ANCHO_SENTIDO`, so the pair reads as an avenue with a median), OSM parks/squares (`suelo`), Google footprints as 1-3 floor grey prisms (`edificio`, floors by a hash of the centroid; skipped when the centroid is on a street, in a green area or in one of the JSON's `reservas`), a barda round the crop and a coloured plug where each street leaves it (`bordes` per side: `gela`, `barda`, `derrumbe`, `camion`). Landmarks are hand pieces (no `auto`). Piece types in `colonia.py`: `caja`, `cilindro`, `edificio` (footprint relative to x/z), `suelo`, `calle` (flat strips on the floor, no collision). Every piece is its own object (Santa Mugre: ~350 pieces, ~670 exported objects with the `-colonly` copies); merging comes with the kit if draw calls need it.
- **Kit and dressing** (`kit.py`, `piezas.py`): houses are `casa` pieces on the facade atlas `textures/mapa/fachadas_kit.png` (the OSM map's 4x4 atlas plus a row of blank walls), house colour as vertex colour (`render_color_index` must be set or the glTF exports white); `MapaPostImport.gd` maps material names to the game's shaders (`fachada_kit` -> `ps1_fachada_kit`, which uses the UVs as set in Blender instead of re-picking tiles; `azotea` -> `ps1_facade`; floors -> `ps1_suelo` with `CAPAS` so roads draw over sidewalks and paths over lawns; `mural` -> `ps1_casa`; `foto_<n>` -> ps1_combined with `textures/mapa/<n>.png`, since the .glb carries no images) and merges meshes in 64 m cells. Details (houses' `detalle_kit` faces: AC units, window bars, antennas, solar heaters, rooftop clotheslines; and every object named `Detalle_*`: alley clotheslines, trash, shrines, hanging bulbs, split into 20 m pieces by `colonia._Trozos`) go into separate 24 m cells with `visibility_range_end` 55 m and no shadows: they nearly doubled the primitives. Godot measures the range to the cell's centre, hence the small cells. The colonia floor is one triangulated mesh (`colonia._piso`, `tessellate_polygon`) with polygon holes for `hundidos` (the sunken pitch, the plaza lawns), each with its own bottom and edge mesh, all with trimesh collision; `terrazas` are raised boxes with retaining walls. Day-only geometry (a piece's `k.dia` Constructor, e.g. the tianguis stalls' tarps and goods) is exported as `Dia_<name>` objects, merged into `Dia_x_y` cells (group `solo_dia`) and hidden at night by `Colonia.gd`. Changing the post-import script doesn't trigger a reimport: change an import setting or delete `.godot/imported/<colonia>.glb-*`.
- **Fill, sidewalks, routes**: `bloqueo.py` fills street fronts (and hand-placed alleys) with rows of narrow houses where no footprint is, every 9th lot a `lote` with life; reserves are tested by polygon overlap (a long alley crosses big footprints without containing their centre). Streets get 15 cm raised sidewalks cut where they cross another roadway, with 30° ramp collision; stairs are ~40° ramps (at 43° bodies barely climbed: `floor_max_angle` is 45°). House collision stops at the roof slab, not the parapet, so roofs are walkable; `rutas` are reserved street fronts rebuilt as 2-storey rows with a stair.
- **Test**: `godot --path . --headless -s tools/mapa/probar_colonia.gd` (spawn, standing on the .glb floor, the first door whose giro has an interior in and out, the yonke, climbing a curb and a route stair to the roof). `tools/mapa/captura_colonia.gd` saves views from Godot and `tools/mapa/medir_colonia.gd` measures FPS, frame time and draw calls at fixed spots, day and night (both need a window). ## La Gotera: tinaco, tiendas de favela y minimapa propio (2026-11-13, tarde)

- **Sin Bodega del Agua**: `favela.py` borra su reserva, casa-local, puerta, negocio `purificadora` y la marca `Bodega_Agua`; el cuarto de garrafones del túnel queda como `Garrafones_Tunel`. El laberinto se reacomoda en el hueco de la reserva.
- **Plaza del Tinaco**: bloque 3×3 de celdas libres elegido por distancia al centro del recorte; `lote` `variante: "tinaco"` (piso de concreto, bancas, macetas y pared baja) y marca `tinaco` con `litros: 50000`, `sin_virgen: true`, `sin_defensa: true`. `TinacoComunitario.armar(litros, con_virgen := true, comunitario := true)` omite mancha, ofrenda, veladoras y grupo de defensa cuando no toca; el tinaco de Santa Mugre sigue igual. La marca del tinaco se dibuja en el minimapa como gota azul.
- **Claros**: los otros 4 son bloques 2×2 de celdas libres, con `lote` `claro: true` y variantes `juegos`/`basquet`/`fonda`/`baldio`/`taller`; `_quitar_casas` quita también tablones y escaleras que quedaban dentro del bloque.
