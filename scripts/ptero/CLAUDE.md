# El pterodáctilo de Juárez (`scripts/ptero/`, JJ 2026-11-22)

A boss in `map_test` with no story behind it (JJ): there are pterodactyls in Ciudad Juárez and this one came down. No Serenol. 8 m wingspan. It lives in the clearing in the centre-west of the map and is there day and night.

- **Model**: Picky the Pterodactyl (Sketchfab, CC-BY, `CREDITS.md`), baked to `models/personajes/ptero_picky/ptero_picky.scn` with its 26 clips (`Fly_*`, `Ground_*`, `Land`, `Take Off`). 520 triangles, PS1 shader from `TiendaPostImport` (group `personajes`). It faces −Z. At scale 1 the finger bones are 7.74 m apart (`ENVERGADURA_MODELO`); `ENVERGADURA` 8 sets the scale. Measured in flight it reads ~8.8 m (the wings stretch).
- **Root motion in the clips** (measured, `JefePtero` header): the flying clips carry the body `VUELO_CUERPO` 4.2 m above the origin; standing, the body is at `CUERPO_PIE` 1.5 m and the feet at 0.1; `Land` lowers the body from 4.9 to 1.5; `Take Off` goes 33 m forward at the end, so it's cut at `DESPEGUE_CORTE` 1.75 s. Air↔ground switches happen with the body at standing height (`_alto` = `CUERPO_PIE`), so nothing jumps. `LOOPS` get `LOOP_LINEAR` in `_ready` (the clips come without it).
- **`JefePtero.gd`**: the fight. Two phases by life (`VIDA` 1500, `FASE_2` at half):
  1. Air: `RONDA` circles the player (`RONDA_RADIO`, `RONDA_ALTO`, `RONDA_VEL`); every `PICADA_CADA` s it stops and screeches (`AVISO`, `Fly_Roar 1`, the tell to roll), dives at where you were (`PICADA`, `PICADA_VEL`, `Fly_Attack 2` from `PICADA_CLIP` m away; claws/head bones within `GARRAS_ALCANCE` of your chest = `GARRAS`), then skims past low (`RASANTE`, body at `PICADA_ALTO`: the machete window) and climbs (`SUBE`). Shot while circling, `ESQUIVA` chance of a `Fly_Dodge` that flips its direction.
  2. At half life it falls (`CAE`, `Fly_Injure 2`, `CAE_VEL`) and fights on the ground: `CAMINA` toward you, `ATACA` with a random `Ground_Attack 1/2/3` (the hit comes from the clip: beak or wing-tip bones within `PICO_ALCANCE` of your chest between 25 % and 75 % of the clip; `PICOTAZO`/`ALETAZO`), every `CARGA_CADA` s from afar `RUGE` (`Ground_Roar 2` at `RUGE_VEL`, the tell) and `CARGA` straight ahead (`CARGA_VEL`, `CARGA_DURA`, `CARGA_DANO`). `HERIDO_CADA` damage taken on the ground staggers it (`Ground_Injure`). Every `VUELO_CADA` s it takes off again, does `PICADAS_POR_VUELO` dives and lands near you (`BAJA`, then `Land`).
  - Killed on the ground, `Ground_Die`; in the air it falls first. Hits: spheres on bones (`ESFERAS`, read live from the skeleton, so they follow every clip). Answers the `"lombrices"` group calls (`golpe_cuerpo`, `query_ray_hit`, `bullet_hit` `DANO_BALA`, `apply_explosion_impulse` `DANO_EXPLOSION`) like the cucarrata, so the machete, the pistol and throwables reach it without touching `player.gd`. Everything stays inside `centro`/`radio` (`_dentro`). Signals `murio`, `desperto`, `cambio_fase`. All numbers are first guesses (`docs/pruebas-balance.md`).
  - **Sounds: pending, JJ will pick them in Splice.** `_sonar(evento)` plays `ptero_<evento>` only if it's in `Sonidos.ARCHIVOS`, so registering the files with those names is enough. Events: `ruge`, `chillido`, `despega`, `picada`, `garras`, `aterriza`, `cae`, `carga`, `picotazo`, `aletazo`, `herido`, `muere`.
- **`PteroClaro.gd`** (node `Pterodactilo` in `map_test.tscn`): the clearing is the big open lot west of the avenue, x −910 to −560, z −195 to 0; `CENTRO` (−736, −95), `RADIO` 85. While you're within `APARECE` 220 m it lies in the middle asleep (`DUERME`, `Ground_Idle`, a screech every ~14 s); within `DESPIERTA` 40 m, or when hit, the fight starts. Fighting and farther than `OLVIDA` 170 m from the centre, it goes away and comes back at full life. Dead: `Misiones.matar_jefe("ptero")`, never again. Floor height by a ray at the centre (0 if nothing's loaded).
- **`BarraPtero.gd`**: its HUD bar (name, life with the half mark, what it does in each phase), only while awake.
- **Safehouse**: Shift+`.` puts it 25 m ahead, already awake, in a 40 m circle around you; again restarts it.
- **Test**: `godot --path . --headless tools/probar_ptero.tscn -- --prueba` (10 checks in `map_test`: appears asleep in the clearing, wakes, flies, wingspan, a dive that hits, falls at half life, fights on the ground, the pistol ray reaches it, dies, never returns; a stand-in takes the hits). Without flags you play it 60 m from the clearing. `-- --video` with Movie Maker records the whole fight with captions and then all 26 clips (convert the `.avi` with the `imageio-ffmpeg` binary: `uv run --with imageio-ffmpeg`).
