# Tinaco defence test (`scenes/defensa_tinaco.tscn`, WIP)

The "Vela del tinaco" mission (`docs/misiones.md` §0) as a standalone scene: one night of `DefensaTinaco.duracion_noche` (210 s, 3:30; JJ found 6 min too long; `-- --noche=<s>` overrides it), in one of three modes (`DefensaTinaco.modo`, `-- --modo=facil|medio|dificil`, default `medio`; see Hordes) from 22:00 to 6:00 at Parque Jardín Imperial (Parques del Mosco, `DefensaTinaco.CENTRO` (163, -515)) on the real map. The yellow-arrow entry from `map_test`, one barricade per colonia in a random spot each night, and the trip back come later.

- **Map piece** (`scripts/defensa/DefensaTinaco.gd`): the same nodes as `map_test` (Mapa, Casas, Puntos, Bardas, Arboles, Alumbrado, Pasto, the 1900 m Suelo) targeting a fixed `Centro` node, with radii of 150/220 m. Stores come from `user://puntos_defensa.json`, written in `_enter_tree` with `interactivo` false, so they're closed. Invisible walls at ±50 m are on layer 5 (`PARED_CAPA`), which only the player has in its mask. `Ciclo` is disabled until `_esperar_mapa` has seen `CargaCuadro` idle for `MAPA_QUIETO` frames. Then the tinaco and player go to the ground and the barricade is generated, and only then does the night start.
- **Barricade** (`scripts/defensa/Barricada.gd`): two staggered "rings" generated at random, as if the neighbours had thrown them together in a hurry. `generar(semilla)`: 0 = random, G regenerates, `--semilla=N` repeats one, and the seed and skipped-piece count print to the console.
  - Each ring is an irregular polygon of straight strips (`ANILLOS`). The outer one has few sides, uneven radii and big kinks, or it looked round. Strips are filled from a shuffled bag weighted by `MEZCLA`, with `DESORDEN_*` and `TRASLAPE`.
  - It's **planned first, then each piece is checked**: a ray finds the ground, and the piece is skipped if its box (`CHOCA_*`) touches anything on layer 1. That way houses, walls and trees stand in for the barricade there. Barrels are checked the same way.
  - Gaps are ~4 m (`ANILLOS` `hueco`) and the inner-gap barrel sits to one side. Narrower diagonal gaps were closed on `CampoFlujo`'s 1.25 m grid, so zombies piled up outside.
  - Everything is ~1 m high (`ESCALA` 1.15).
  - Lying pallets behind standing ones collide as a ridge with ~30° sides (`_rampa`, `RAMPA_CAIDA`), not a box: the 33 cm box was a step zombies couldn't climb and `CampoFlujo` (probing 1 m up) didn't see, so they got stuck there.
  - The ~850 sacks are one MultiMesh with a code-drawn paper texture (`_material_costal`). The pallets and metal shelf are built in code.
  - The portable toilet (`models/props/bano_portatil`) lies on its side. Barrels are `MapAlumbrado.armar_tambos` plus an `OmniLight3D` each.
  - The tinaco (`models/props/tinaco`, keeping only `TINACO_PIEZAS`) is 2.8 m (10,000 L).
- **Tinaco** (`scripts/defensa/TinacoDefensa.gd`, built by `Barricada._tinaco`): group `tinaco_defensa`, `VIDA` 300, `radio`, `recibir_golpe`, signals `cambio`/`cayo`/`aviso`. Jets (`tinaco_chorro`) appear as it loses life.
  - The design doc says 100 life, but at 2 per second per zombie a horde broke it in seconds.
  - **If it breaks, the night is cut**: fade to black, `Ciclo.amanecer_ya(true)`, and **no pay**. Half pay was abusable: enter, lose on purpose, get paid (JJ's decision).
- **Zombies**: `Zombis.espontaneos` false (no loose ones or Director mobs), `lejos` 150, `max_total` 40 (30 elsewhere; it also sizes the pool) and `dano_jugador` 6 (12 elsewhere, `Zombie.DANO`; the boss keeps `JEFE_DANO`).
  - Only the machete swing and hit, the pistol shot and the zombie roar (capped by `Sonidos.turno`) sound, plus the music: `DefensaTinaco.SONIDOS` goes into `Sonidos.solo` (any other name returns a null stream) and is cleared in `_exit_tree`.
  - `-- --monitor=<dir>` adds `scripts/defensa/MonitorDefensa.gd`: every 5 s of real time a row in `monitor.csv` (hour, alive, group size, who's attacking what, both lives, damage dealt by melee and pistol, damage taken, healing, swings and shots with hits, intensity, FPS), hits and heals in `eventos.txt`, and `resumen.txt` at the end. Counters: `Zombie.dano_cuerpo`/`dano_pistola` (static), `Player.golpes`/`golpes_pegaron`, `Pistola.tiros`/`aciertos`. On Hyprland, run with `--display-driver wayland`: under XWayland the captured mouse escapes the window.
  - Each zombie picks an outer-ring gap on waking (`Zombie._escoger_hueco`: `HUECO_CERCANO` 40 % the nearest, otherwise another at random; runners too, since sending them all to the nearest made them hit the tinaco at once). It walks there through its own `CampoFlujo` (`Zombis.campos_huecos`, one per `Barricada.huecos_mundo`, set by `Zombis.usar_huecos` after each `generar`; `CampoFlujo.una_vez` fills them once since the goals don't move) with `_blanco` null, and within `HUECO_LLEGA` switches to the tinaco, or as soon as it's `HUECO_ADENTRO` closer to the tinaco than its gap (the shortest path to a far gap can cut through the yard, and they jammed against the inner ring). Otherwise they all took the nearest gap and one player could plug it.
  - `Zombie._destino` goes for the tinaco through `Zombis.campo_tinaco` (metas on a ring around it). It switches to the player only within `_persigue()` **and** when the player is closer than the tinaco, so standing beside the tinaco doesn't protect it. Once the tinaco breaks it goes for the player.
  - Reach adds the tinaco's `radio`.
  - `Zombis.olvidar_paredes()` (`CampoFlujo.olvidar()`) after regenerating.
  - None of this changes `map_test` or the safehouse.
- **Hordes** (`scripts/defensa/Hordas.gd`): groups, no long pauses (Alan and JJ's feedback, tuned by play-testing with the monitor). `GRUPOS` gives the group size per hour (interpolated, with a runner fraction: 3 at 22:00, 7 at midnight, 7 with runners at 3:00, 7 at 5:00; 9 jammed the end). A group spawns all at once (`CADA` 0.2 s apart) from one side, never where the camera sees (`Zombis.a_la_vista`, the same frustum test as `_lugar_libre`), or two opposite sides from `DE_DOS_LADOS`, 41-46 m out, 41-46 m out, so it takes ~10-15 s to arrive: pistol time, then the machete. The next one leaves when `QUEDAN` 1 is left, after at least `ESPERA_MIN`; after `ESPERA_MAX` 20 s it leaves anyway, but only if fewer than its size are alive, so at most two groups overlap. Refilling as each died (the first version) kept zombies on you all the time and there was never time for the pistol. Nothing after `ULTIMA` (5:30). **Modes** (`Hordas.MODOS`, applied by `usar_modo`): `facil` = `GRUPOS_FACIL` (7 at the end) with `tope` (the `ESPERA_MAX` release only when fewer than the group are alive) and runners spread over the gaps; `medio` = `GRUPOS_MEDIO` (9 at 5:00), no `tope`, runners to the nearest gap (`Zombis.corredores_al_cercano`), JJ's pick after tests 11 and 13; `dificil` = medio × `dificultad` 1.3, not played yet.
  - **Tinaco cap** (`Zombis.cupo_tinaco`, 8 in `defensa_tinaco.tscn`, 0 elsewhere): when a wave zombie spawns with `Zombis.al_tinaco()` already at the cap it gets `Zombie.por_jugador` and hunts the player like an ambusher; slots refill as tinaco-goers die. `medio` groups are ×1.25 (`dificultad`), `dificil` ×1.6.
  - **El Panzón** (`Hordas._panzon`): from 0:00 each in-game hour rolls `MODOS["panzon"]` (fácil 0, medio 0.35, difícil 0.6), never two alive, and one is forced at `PANZON_SEGURO` 3:00 if none came (medio/difícil). It spawns through `_lugar` (off camera) and counts toward `muertos`.
    - `DefensaTinaco` shows a red "EL PANZÓN" bar top centre while one lives.
    - His rocks knock down barricade pieces: `Barricada.romper_cerca(punto, radio)` frees the pieces' StaticBody3D (not the tambos or the tinaco), hides their MultiMesh instances and calls `Zombis.olvidar_paredes()`, so the flow field sees the hole. `golpe` and `morir_zombi` were added to `SONIDOS`.
  - **Ambushes** (`Hordas._emboscadas`, AG's addition; not in `facil`): outside the wave loop, every `EMBOSCADA_CADA` 35-60 s from `EMBOSCADA_DESDE` 23:00 until `ULTIMA`, 2-3 hunters (`EMBOSCADA_GRUPO`) spawn on the player's side of the ring through `_lugar` (off camera), with no `gemido`. `Zombie.emboscada` skips the tinaco branch of `_destino`, so they go for the player. They don't count toward `QUEDAN` for the next wave (`_emboscados`), and each one prints with its time.
  - L4D-style relief: `Zombis.director.intensidad` (raised by damage and nearby kills) decays here, and at `TOPE` delays the next group by `RESPIRO` 6 s.
  - Spawns go through `Zombis.sacar`, capped at `max_total`. Rooftop spots are rejected and a blocked side is swapped.
  - No text warning: a low, filtered `gemido` (`AudioStreamPlayer3D`) plays where each group spawns (muted while `Sonidos.solo` is set).
  - `dificultad` (1 in the pilot) multiplies the group size: later nights should only be winnable with better weapons. It also counts `muertos` and the peak (`mas_juntos`) for the end screen. `hora()` maps `Ciclo.tiempo()` to 22.0-30.0, reaching 6:00 at `amanecio`.
- **HUD and end**: clock, dawn bar and tinaco bar top left, under the player's life bar, in Pixelify Sans (`fonts/`, OFL, through `resources/ui/tema_pixel.tres`; only the defence uses it for now). The neighbours lend the school backpack and top you up to `PROVISIONES` 5 healing items.
  - `_pantalla_final(gano, desmayo)` pauses and animates on its own always-processing layer: the title drops and bounces (win) or shakes in red (loss), the lines fade in one by one with a sound, the pay counts up with `moneda` and a pixelfx `premio_monedas`, then a random closing line. pixelfx sheets are drawn inside the screen by `_hoja` (a tween over `EfectosPixel.hoja_de`, so they run while paused): god rays on a win, two `tinaco_chorro` jets on a loss. Win pays `PAGA` 150 plus `galon_agua`; losing pays nothing. Buttons: Otra noche / Salir (to `inicio.tscn`).
  - **Fainting ends the mission** (JJ's call; crawling and reviving are left for coop): `Supervivencia.desmayo_final` makes `_desmayarse` turn the TV off and call it instead of respawning or dropping a bag.
  - M toggles the music (`_musica_apagada`, `stream_paused`): `InventoryUI.con_mapa` is false in this scene, so the inventory leaves `inventory_map` alone and drops "M: Mapa" from its hint.
  - Music: `audio/musica/pt_tema.mp3` loops (`loop=true` in its `.import`, also forced in code), fades in when the night starts and out when it ends, and keeps playing under the paused inventory.
  - Zombis and InventoryUI are as in the safehouse, and so are JJ's sword tests: `_ready` turns on `Player.golpe_cargado` (the heavy slash) and `Player.giro_aereo`, and `_exit_tree` turns them off. Everything else (combo with `_Rec`, buffer, roll, block, bare-hand combo) is always on.
- **Minimap**: `MapMinimapa` with `datos_path`/`puntos_path` empty, `zombis_siempre` and `centro_fijo` at `CENTRO` (`radio_metros` 50). It still turns with the camera and the player arrow moves.
  - `_dibujar_mapa` crops the real `minimapa.png` to ±60 m and doubles it to 0.5 m/px. It darkens outside the walls and paints `Barricada.huellas`, the barrels and the tinaco.
- `-- --capturas=<dir>` saves the `VISTAS` screenshots plus `minimapa.png` and quits (needs a window). A sped-up full night was tested with a scratch scene (`Engine.time_scale` 4): undefended, the tinaco falls around 23:20.
