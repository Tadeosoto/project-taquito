# La Madre del Cárcamo: the worm boss (`scripts/lombrices/`, AG's proposal, branch `lombriciento`)

A boss built on the Hitman ragdoll trick: Verlet dots joined by distance constraints, no colliders. Lore proposal in `docs/lore.md` §16 (Serenol sludge in GelA cisterns breeds worms; not yet voted, `revision-historia` 4.12). Numbers to tune: `docs/pruebas-balance.md`.

**Solver** (`SolverLombrices`, RefCounted, no nodes; one entry point, GDExtension-ready):
- `step(pos, old_pos, links, vivos, modo, meta, uniones, dt, campo)`. Byte `links[i]` joins i→i+1. Extra `modo` bits `LEJOS`/`DORMIDA` let far worms move one step in `LEJOS_CADA` with that longer dt. `uniones` holds extra pairs (fused whips).
- Each dot has `modo` bits (`CABEZA`, `BAJO_TIERRA`: ignores the field and gravity, goes straight to its target under the floor; `ANCLA`: pinned to `meta`; `LIBRE`: no steering; `AIRE`: steers without contact, low gravity; `RAPIDO`: double speed) and a target `meta`.
- A step: Verlet with steering toward `meta` for dots touching a surface; on walls gravity drops by `AGARRE` and they climb when the target is behind or above. Then 3 rounds of links + unions and field push-out (repulsion only in the first round), then the player capsule and the field box.
- Repulsion uses a hashed grid: linear key `x + 73y + 5867z`, 16384 slots, linked lists, the 13 forward cells. Each dot reads the field once per step (inlined in `_integrar`) and uses that plane during the rounds.
- `radio`/`largo`/`velocidad`/`separar` are per instance: the Madre uses the same class with fat dots.

**Distance field** (`CampoDistancia`):
- Baked at spawn inside `caja` (29×6×29 m in the arena, ~9 s; 32×5×32 in the safehouse), `celda` 0.25 m, `ABAJO` 1 m under the floor so the floor lands on a cell edge.
- Occupancy from `intersect_shape` (layer 1, player excluded, 4³ blocks first), then nearest-seed propagation up to `LIMITE` 6 cells each way. Budgeted at 6 ms per frame: ~3-4 s (~9.5 s for the arena's bigger box).
- **Saved field**: with `EnjambreLombrices.guardar_campo` set (the arena: `user://campo_carcamo.bin`, ~100 KB, zstd), the baked `datos` are written after the first bake and loaded on later runs (~1.4 s from scene start instead of ~11). The file carries `CampoDistancia.firma`, a hash of every layer-1 collision shape (class, size, transform; not characters or areas) touching the box, so any geometry change rebakes on its own. The safehouse leaves it empty.

**Swarm** (`EnjambreLombrices`, group `lombrices`), 80 × 12 dots:
- **Performance** (AG got 6 FPS in the editor when the main thread waited on a >16 ms step every 60 Hz tick and Godot ran extra ticks to catch up):
  - The step runs on `WorkerThreadPool` at `hz` 30 and the main thread never waits for it. If it isn't done, that tick only redraws.
  - Each tick draws interpolated between the last two snapshots (`_antes` → `vista`).
  - Weapon mutations made while the worker runs are queued in `_ordenes` and applied when the step is collected. Hits are detected at once on `vista`, so `golpe_cuerpo` still returns immediately.
  - `_esperar` (blocking) is only for `soltar`/`rearmar`/`_exit_tree`.
  - Per-step rates (damping, steering) scale with dt, so any `hz` looks the same.
  - The field sample is inlined in `_integrar`, repulsion runs only in the first of the 3 rounds, and RODEA worms past `LEJOS_DESDE` 14 m step one in three (`LEJOS`/`DORMIDA` bits).
  - Auto quality: if the step averages over `CALIDAD_ALTA` 22 ms for 2 s, the farthest worms are removed silently, down to `MINIMO` 40.
  - The crías cast no shadows.
  - Measured from the terminal on AG's machine: ~8.7 ms per step on the worker, ~1 ms think + ~1.3 ms draw on the main thread. With `-- --lento=40` (a fake 40 ms step) the arena still runs at 144 FPS.
  - F3 or `-- --perfil` in the arena prints per-phase µs (`solver.perfil`).
  - Thinking runs on the main thread with the step collected.
- A worm is any run of live linked dots (`_tramos`: `_cabeza_de`, `_cola`); a cut half is a new worm. Halves regrow one dot every `CRECE_CADA` s from dead dots of their own 12-slot block, so the total never passes 960.
- States per head (`Estado`):
  - RODEA: a slot on a ring around where the player **will be** (velocity × up to `ADELANTA_MAX` 1.2 s) that closes in. Random 0.5 s bursts (`RAPIDO`, `RAPIDO_X` 1.75: 4 → 7 m/s). Every 6-10 s, if 15+ are within 8 m, all nearby burst together for 2 s (an "ola").
  - BOLA (phase 2+, every `BOLA_CADA` 15-25 s, one at a time): 8-12 RODEA worms 4-14 m away knot into a ball.
    - Every dot is ANCLA'd to a spot on a rotating Fibonacci sphere (`_colocar_bola`), heads 1.3× out, wiggling.
    - It gathers for `BOLA_JUNTA` 1 s, rolls on the floor at `BOLA_VEL` 5 m/s with a small `madre_niebla` aura, then pounces the last 3 m in a 0.45 s arc for `BOLA_DANO` 18 (+ knockback, roll-dodgeable). On landing: `torre_golpe` and dirt.
    - Hits on member dots lower `BOLA_VIDA` 40 instead of cutting (machete damage, pistol 5, salt 8 per tick; fire kills its members outright; the charged slash breaks it at once).
    - Breaking it or landing scatters the members `ATONTADA` 1.5 s: no steering, a free window to cut them.
  - SUBE: when the player stands more than `SUBE_DESDE` 1.2 m above a worm, it picks one of the scene's `rampas` (`[foot, top]` pairs; the arena registers `CarcamoNivel.rampas`: two ramps and two stairs) by cost: its distance to the foot, plus top-to-player (Manhattan with `anillo`), plus `CARGA_RAMPA` 1.5 m per worm already climbing it, so 80 worms spread over all four instead of jamming the nearest (measured: 36/18/11/15), crawls up and only leaves the route past the ramp's top. Up there, worms at the player's level go straight at them (no ring, so they don't fall off), except that with `anillo` set (the arena's mezzanine centreline half-size) a worm on another side of the square ring first heads for the corner of its side that gives the shorter path (`_por_anillo`), instead of straight across the hole.
    - With no ramps, it uses `CampoDistancia.trepaderos` (floor points at the foot of solid columns) filtered by `CampoDistancia.plataforma` (the solid layer under the player's feet, flood-filled), so they climb a pillar that touches the player's platform and not the opposite wall. Pillars that pass through a catwalk trap them under it, hence the ramps.
    - The sideways wave `ONDA` is 0.35 (0.7 threw them off narrow surfaces).
  - TREPA → ACECHA (phase 2+): up to 3 worms next to a wall or pillar (field probed sideways at mid height) climb to `TREPA_ALTO` and wait. When the player passes within 3 m they drop on them (as a SALTA: bite + latch).
  - ERGUIDA → SALTA: from `SALTO_DESDE` 4.5 m, a 0.45 s rear-up tell with the maw open, then a leap. A hit damages and, unless blocking, the worm latches.
  - PEGADA: anchored to a point in the player's `frijol` space. Each one slows (`Player.lastres`) and drains life. A roll throws them off; Q heal or salt burns them.
  - ENTERRADA (phase 2+): dives, travels to behind the player and bursts out into a leap.
  - LATIGO/COLA (phase 2+): 3-5 worms joined by `uniones`, the leader sweeping a circle.
  - TORRE → CAE (phase 3): ~40 % climb into a column over a faint growing shadow, then drop for `TORRE_DANO`.
  - INFECTA: rides a zombie; `Zombie.infectar()` makes it a faster hunter.
- Crawl speed `SolverLombrices.VELOCIDAD` 4 m/s (2.2 was easy to walk around). Each step's displacement is capped at `TOPE_PASO` 0.3 m so fast dots don't tunnel.
- **Pixel effects** (`_fx`, with per-sheet minimum gaps in `FX_ESPERA` so a 12-dot slash isn't 12 sheets): `cria_corte` + a quiet `squelch` on cuts, `cria_cabeza` on whole-worm kills, `pegada_mordida`/`pegada_suelta` on latch and roll-off, `madre_tierra` (small) when burrowers erupt, `torre_golpe` + `retumbo` on the tower drop. Salt circles loop `sal_burbujas` and play `fizz`; LODO zones (the hidden pool slow; spit no longer leaves one) loop `lodo_charco`; Molotovs burst with `molotov_revienta`, spit with `lodo_choque`.
- `fase` 1-3 gates these (3 when there is no Madre). `parir()` revives dead runs as new worms flying from a point. `disolver()` kills everything.
- **Weapons**:
  - `query_ray_hit(origin, dir, max_dist, radius)`, heads no bigger than bodies. `bullet_hit`: a head kills the whole worm; a body hit splits it and pushes nearby dots through `old_pos`.
  - `golpe_cuerpo(origen, frente, alcance, cono, dano, giro, todas)`: cuts the nearest `dano × CORTES_POR_DANO` 0.34 (machete: 4); `todas` (the charged tajo) cuts up to `CORTES_TAJO` 12. Latched worms are always in reach. With the fire trail styles (`EstelaBarrido` FUEGO/FLAMAS_FELIPE) it burns the worm's head instead of splitting it.
  - `apply_explosion_impulse(origin, radius, force, mata)`: the aerial spin landing calls it with `mata` false (`Player.AZOTE_*`).
  - Static `rayo` checks everything in the group; `explotar_en_mira` is a test hook.
  - `Pistola.disparar` and `Player._golpear`/`_pegar_estocada` call these untyped, so the Madre answers too.
- **Look**: two MultiMeshes from `MallaLombriz`, a 6-sided tube per link (tapered, peristalsis) and a lamprey maw per head (teeth open via `INSTANCE_CUSTOM.r`). Since JJ's look pass the crías share the Madre's look: `shaders/madre.gdshader` through `MallaLombriz.material_crias()` (its own material, so `EnjambreLombrices` raises `brillo` at night without touching the Madre), plus a third MultiMesh of small dorsal spines (`MallaLombriz.espina()`, one every other link, along the worm's own up so they follow climbs and towers). The crías' material sets `linea_lomo` 1: a thin glowing blue line along the back from head to tail (local +Z of each `MallaLombriz.tramo` tube, `step(0.982, …)`), so they read apart (JJ); with it the crías have no cracks at all, on the body or the spines (the Madre keeps hers). `MallaLombriz._anillos` used to wind its tubes inward, so every worm (crías and Madre) showed its inside faces; it now faces outward. With that fixed the maw looked hollow, so the head's inward lip is also built facing the axis (`_anillos(..., adentro)`, flat-shaded so its normals don't cancel the outer one) and the throat disc is two-sided; on the Madre (`linea_lomo` 0) the throat glows as a pixel-stepped circle, near-white blue at the centre to the cracks' blue at the edge, breathing with the rest (JJ). `shaders/lombriz.gdshader` (the old flesh with ring bands and turquoise veins) is no longer used. Blood is dirty water + dark red GPU pixels plus `Liquido` splats; a brown pool shows dirt.

**La Madre** (`MadreCarcamo`, in the `lombrices` group):
- 18 dots on her own solver, on the main thread, living in `charco` (under the floor: `BAJO_TIERRA`). **`ESCALA` 1.5** (JJ, 2026-11-09: twice was too big) multiplies her dot radius (0.42 → 0.63 m), link length (0.5 → 0.75 m, so she's ~13.5 m long), body thickness `GRUESO`, sac radius and height, bite and leap heights, burial depth (-0.55 × ESCALA), the rearing pose and the mouth; the melee height check is 1 m + `RADIO`.
- 6 glowing `SACOS` (`SACO_VIDA` 70 each, radius 0.24 m × `ESCALA`, no regen: AG found regen a chore) are the only weak points.
  - Hittable only while she is really out: TIRADA, BARRE, rearing in VOMITA/ESCUPE, or ATURDIDA. Bullets do 5 (on the sacs only) and fire 2 per 0.5 s; in melee only the charged slash counts (below). Her body blocks bullets.
  - **Look** (JJ, after Godzilla Minus One's tail): `shaders/madre.gdshader`, charcoal keloid skin (`COLOR` #3F4447, Voronoi cells on the pixel grid) with cyan Serenol cracks (#2EC4FF) brighter toward the tail (`INSTANCE_CUSTOM.g`; `.a` seeds each link's skin), and blue-tipped dorsal spines (`MallaLombriz.espina()`, skipped on links that carry a fin). Everything that glows breathes together, Madre and crías alike: `aliento()` in the shader, `respiracion` 0.25 Hz = 15 breaths a minute, on the global `TIME`.
  - Each sac is drawn as a flat fin like a spinosaur's (`MallaLombriz.aleta()`: wide along the body, thin sideways, tip swept toward the tail), centred on the back and sized by the body's thickness at that link (small toward the tail); the hit sphere stays at `_saco(k)`. Same skin and cracks as the body, cracks going from the body's blue to the sac colour up the fin (`es_saco`, `color_azul`), tip lit in the sac colour: magenta #E040FB → pink #FF4FA8 → orange #FF7A45 by life, pulsing faster (`LATE`) as it empties.
  - Damaged sacs drip `Liquido` (magenta + dark red, faster when low) and leave a floor trail while she drags. Every hit spurts along the hit direction with the `saco_golpe` sheet and the `squelch` sound.
  - **Popping a sac** plays `saco_revienta` + a big splash + a floor stain, and she collapses for `ATURDIDA_DURA` 2 s, sacs exposed. The next sac pulses brighter: a combo window. A phase change also plays `fase_onda` + `chillido`.
- Attack cycle, resting in the pool `DESCANSO` s between:
  - MORDIDA: chases you underground, then a 0.6 s tell (small dirt puffs and the low `retumbo`; no ring: AG wanted subtle cues), a vertical bite through the floor (`madre_tierra` sheet), then lies on the floor `TIRADA`. Every other attack starts with a `madre_surge` geyser and `chapoteo`. Death plays `madre_muere` bursts and leaves a `madre_niebla` loop for 4 s.
  - BARRIDO: surfaces and circles the arena.
  - VOMITO: rears up and calls `parir`.
  - ESCUPE: rears up and spits `Arrojadizo` type 3 **like a bullet** (JJ, 2026-11-09: it used to be a slow lob that left a sludge puddle): straight, no gravity, at `ESCUPE_VEL` 14 m/s (~0.7 s from 10 m, time to roll; 30 couldn't be dodged) at your chest with `ESCUPE_ADELANTA` 0.5 lead and `ESCUPE_ERROR` 0.35 m of error; if its ray hits the player it does `ESCUPE_DANO` 10 (`Arrojadizo.dano`, through `recibir_dano`, so rolling dodges it) and bursts with `lodo_choque`; no puddle (9 of 12 hit a player standing still at 30 m/s). Its glob and trail radii scale with `MadreCarcamo.ESCALA`. In flight it's a spit glob, not a ball: a sphere stretched along its velocity (`ESCUPE_ESTIRA`, up to 1.9×) that wobbles, with a trail of `ESCUPE_COLA` droplets (top-level, placed where the glob was 1, 2, 3, 4 frames ago), looking like the Madre's own skin (`shaders/escupitajo.gdshader`, one shared material: near-black navy body with thin hard-edged cracks of moving value noise in the veins' blue #2EC4FF, flickering black bubbles, a faint fresnel rim and a pulse, all on a coarse pixel grid of the sphere direction; JJ found the bright versions too light) with a small blue light.
- In phase 3 she chains a second attack without resting `ENCADENA` 40 % of the time.
- **No safe hits from above** (JJ): `arriba` is true while the player stands (on the floor, so a jump from the ground doesn't count) more than `ARRIBA_DESDE` 1.2 m over the pool level. Then she only rears (ESCUPE 65 %, VOMITA), `_expuesto` is false so sacs take no damage, and stepping up while she's out (VA, AVISA, TIRADA, BARRE, ATURDIDA) makes her dive back at once (`_ver_arriba`, `cuenta.zambullidas`). The crías still climb after you. **She only swims in the pool** (JJ, 2026-11-09: she used to travel under the concrete): with `alberca` set (`CarcamoNivel.ALBERCA`; 0 in the safehouse keeps the old free roaming), VA goes under the Serenol to the edge nearest you (`_dentro`, `ORILLA` 0.9 m in), warns there and, if you're on the concrete, SALTA leaps at you in an arc (`SALTA_DURA` 0.75 s, `SALTA_ALTO` 2.2 m, first `SALTA_PUNTOS` 8 points in the arc, bites for `DANO_MORDIDA`), lands TIRADA; in the pool she still bites from below. Out of the pool, `_descansar`/`_ver_arriba` call `_volver`: REGRESA crawls her across the floor until head and mid-body are in the pool (`REGRESA_MAX` 7 s), then she dives. SALTA and REGRESA are exposed states. **She climbs over the pool railing** (JJ): the railing is on layer 2, which her field doesn't see, but the pit's 0.9 m wall stopped her heavy body, so BARRE never left the pool; `_trepar` turns every crawling point inside the rim band (`TREPA_ADENTRO` 1 m inside the edge to `TREPA_AFUERA` 0.6 m past the railing, `barandal`/`barandal_a` from `CarcamoNivel`) into AIRE with its goal raised over the railing, in BARRE, REGRESA and the tail of SALTA. **BARRE is now a charge** (JJ, 2026-11-09: circling the big room there was never anything to dodge): `EMBISTE_PASADAS` 2/3/3 passes, each a `EMBISTE_APUNTA` 0.4 s tell (head raised toward you, a quiet `retumbo`) then a straight dash at `EMBISTE_VEL` 1.6× speed to where you were plus `EMBISTE_PASA` 3 m (`_apuntar_embestida`, clamped to `EMBISTE_HASTA` 11 m so she stays out from under the mezzanine), ending within `EMBISTE_LLEGA` or after `EMBISTE_MAX` 3 s; roll sideways. Measured headless from the pool to a player 9 m away: ~2.3 s including the climb. `VOMITA_CRIAS` is 6 (+ phase) per mouthful (was 3, JJ). She can only revive dead slots of the swarm's fixed 80 × 12 pool, so a vomit into a full swarm brings nothing. After the first 4 turns VOMITA is a random 1 in 4, so clearing the crías buys time to shoot her (JJ wants that; forcing a vomit when few were left was tried and reverted). **What hurts her** (JJ, 2026-11-09, replacing body hits for everything): bullets only on the crests (the sacs; a body hit just bleeds a little, `bullet_hit`), and in melee only the charged slash (`golpe_cuerpo` with `todas`, i.e. `Player._tajo`): it hurts every sac it reaches and, through the body, each section it reaches once (`_seccion(i)`: the live sac whose `SACO_EN` is nearest to body point `i`; a popped sac's section passes to its neighbours) for `DANO_CUERPO` (1.0) × the damage, on exposed points only (`_punto_expuesto`). Normal swings, the aerial spin and the air thrust still connect (`golpe_cuerpo` returns true) but do nothing. Molotov fire still hurts sacs and sections in it. Every 2 sacs popped raise `enjambre.fase`; 6 popped make her thrash and die (`muerta`). `BarraMadre` is the HUD: sac bar with phase marks and a swarm biomass bar, in Pixelify.

**Counter-play**:
- `ZonaLombrices` (group `zonas_lombriz`, `crear` sets the position before `add_child`, or the fire was built at the origin, in the pool): SAL eats a head per worm every `SAL_CADA` 0.8 s and repels; FUEGO kills every cría with a dot inside it at once, ball members too (JJ; it used to burn one tail dot every 0.25 s), hurts sacs and you; REPELE is a burning barrel (group `tambos_lombriz`), and melee kicks it over into fire (`patear`); LODO slows through `Player.lastres`.
- Salt, the `Lanzador`, the Molotov chain, the palomita, the bocina and the gasolinera are in `docs/sistemas/arrojables.md`.

**Grounded effects**: one-shot slots with `"suelo": true` (dirt, geyser, sac pop, spit splash, shockwave, death, tower dust, Molotov, worm poof) use `BILLBOARD_FIXED_Y` and no `ACERCAR` push, so their base stays on the floor seen from above. Loops take `de_pie` (`material_bucle`/`cuadro_bucle`, a `sprite_pixel.gdshader` uniform: turns only around Y). The Madre's sheets fill their frames, so their slot `escala` is low (0.3-0.4); at 1.0 the dirt was 11 m tall.

**Sheets**: the 16 Serenol-palette sheets (turquoise/teal, sick yellow, dark red, murky brown) are pixelfx library effects recolored in `tools/pixelfx/efectos.json` (`madre_*`, `saco_*`, `lodo_*`, `fase_onda`, `cria_*`, `sal_burbujas`, `molotov_revienta`, `torre_golpe`, `pegada_*`). Big ones are rendered at 12-15 fps. One-shots are `EfectosPixel.LUGARES` slots, all in `EXTRAS`, so F9 turns them off; loops go through `cuadro_bucle`. New generated sounds in `Sonidos`: `squelch`, `fizz`, `chillido`, `chapoteo`.

**Arena** (`scenes/lombrices.tscn`, `PruebaLombrices.gd`):
- The cárcamo is a GelA pumping room with a pool-room look, built in code by `CarcamoNivel.gd` (JJ's design, 2026-11-09): 28×28 m, 10 m walls (7 let you jump through the ceiling from the mezzanine; the ceiling now has collision) painted white with random mould and damp (`shaders/pared_moho.gdshader`: world-space noise, denser near the floor, under the mezzanine and the ceiling, plus drip streaks; no decals), a grey concrete floor (seamless noise textures from `_textura_ruido`, triplanar, a casting joint every 2.4 m), a 9×9 m pool 0.9 m deep that looks bottomless: its sides and floor fade from concrete to black (`shaders/alberca_hondo.gdshader`, by world height) under dark Serenol (`shaders/serenol_alberca.gdshader` at `AGUA`: reads the depth texture, so what's just under the surface shows and anything `desvanece` 0.8 m deeper is lost in black; the Madre's buried end never shows) with a hazard-striped edge and a yellow railing with two ladder gaps (north and south), a perimeter mezzanine at `MEZZ_ALTO` 3.5 m (2.5 m wide, on yellow posts, walkable underneath; metal grating `_rejilla`, an alpha-scissor texture, over an invisible collision slab, with steel beams under it; ramps and steps use the same grating) with its own railing, entered through a door in the north wall (`ENTRADA`, already up). Down by two ramps (west and east, along the mezzanine's inner edge, 7 m long) or two stairs (south, 40°: an invisible slope with visual steps). Industrial signs (`_letrero`, Label3D), the GelA drop logo only (`textures/logos-negocios/gela-gota.svg`, recoloured to blue in `_gota_azul`, as Decals), pipes and a green SALIDA sign over the door. **At night and nearly dark** (JJ): of the 9 hanging lamps most are burnt out, one stays on dimly (over the entry) and two flicker with `Alumbrado.parpadeo` like the street posts and El Punto's bulb, with `MapAlumbrado.armar_chispas` sparks (the middle one is a shadowed spot over the pool; `_titilan`, `_process`); blue ambient `CarcamoNivel.ambiente` 0.15 (0.8 was for screenshots; JJ then asked for darker, creepier), lit lamps at 0.45 and fog 0.035. **Batching**: `_juntar` merges every static `MeshInstance3D` with a `PrimitiveMesh` into one mesh per material (SurfaceTool `append_from`; collision untouched), except those with meta `suelta` (the flickering bulbs, the exit sign); the grating is its own batch in group `carcamo_rejilla`. ~400 draw calls became ~105 and the GPU frame went from ~10.3 to ~7.8 ms on JJ's PC (also fewer lights). Signs share materials through `_liso`. The pool railing is on collision layer 2 (the arena adds it to the player's mask) so the distance field doesn't see it and worms leave the pool without climbing it; the mezzanine railing is layer 1. The pool is a hidden `ZonaLombrices` LODO (`ver` false) that slows you. 4 barrels on the deck, one salt and one Molotov pickup in the south corners under the mezzanine, refilled on every phase change.
