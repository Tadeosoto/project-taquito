# La cucarrata (`scripts/cucarrata/`, JJ's boss, design in progress, 2026-11-17)

A giant cockroach with a rat's head and blue Serenol eyes, built in code from two Sketchfab models (CC-BY, in `CREDITS.md`): `models/personajes/cucarrata/cucaracha.glb` (skeleton + 6 clips) and `rata.glb` (static mesh). No lore entry or mission yet; vida, attacks and arena are still to be designed by JJ.

- **`Cucarrata.gd`**: removes the cockroach's head triangles (vertices weighted mostly to `head_04`; `preparar_cucaracha`), cuts the rat's head (`RATA_CORTE_*`), caps the neck hole (`_tapar_cuello`) and hangs it from `head_04` with a `BoneAttachment3D`. `animar(corto)` with `ANIMS` names (quieta, camina, ataca, muere, vuela, agarra, pare).
- **Skin**: `shaders/cucarrata.gdshader`, the Madre del Cárcamo's keloids and cracks (`madre.gdshader`) adapted to a skinned mesh: cells on the UV (object space would slide while animating), breathing on the global `TIME` at the Madre's 0.25 Hz. Body brown (`CUERPO_COLOR`), rat head stone grey like the Panzón. Antennae (`horns_*` bones) are marked in vertex `COLOR` (g = antenna, r = base→tip) and glow in segments toward the tip (`antena_*` uniforms).
- **`parir(tipos)`**: plays "pare", built in code over "idle" (`_armar_pare`: the abdomen bone `body_02.001_036` rises on its local −X, swells and pushes at `PARE_PUJOS`) and releases a swarm: `POR_PUJO[tipo]` (3 rats, 2 cockroaches: JJ halved them) `CriaCucarrata` of the push's type per push, `ENJAMBRE_CADA` s apart, spread sideways (`ENJAMBRE_ABRE`) with varied speeds, from `punta_cola()` into the cucarrata's parent. Each birth is one type only and they alternate (JJ: you can't defend against both at once): `parir()` with no list births a `TANDA` of `siguiente_tipo` (3 pushes of rats = 9, or 2 of cockroaches = 4) and flips it. Signal `pario`. Size: `alto` (3 m without antennae, JJ) sets the scale over `ALTO_MODELO` 0.58 m; the eye lights' range is scaled by hand.
- **`CriaCucarrata.gd`**, children with their own crack cells (a scaled-down boss would have millimetre cracks): rats chihuahua-sized, cockroaches three times that (~1.35 m, JJ: easier to shoot; hits use `centro()` and `RADIO_CUCARACHA`):
  - Cucaracha (brown): kamikaze. Flies horizontal (the glb's flying pose is upright, so the model is pitched −90°), each one flies to its own slot around the player (golden-angle spread, `RONDA_RADIO`, its own height, slowly circling at `RONDA_GIRA`) and attacks one at a time (`ATACAN_JUNTAS` 1, `ATAQUE_CADA` s between starts; static `_atacando`): when its turn comes it brakes and hangs in the air for `APUNTA_DURA` while turning upright and facing the player (the tell to roll), then accelerates upright at the player's chest with limited steering (`PICADA_GIRA`), and `explotar()`s in Serenol blue (`_estallido` sphere + light, `saco_revienta`) for `EXPLOTA_DANO` within `EXPLOTA_RADIO` through `Player.recibir_dano` (rolling dodges, blocking reduces).
  - Rata (grey): runs at the player, leaps from `SALTO_DESDE`, bites (`MORDIDA`) and latches at a spot in the player's `frijol` space, slowing (`Player.lastres["cucarrata_ratas"]`) and draining `CHUPA`/s. Only the machete removes it: every swing (`golpe_cuerpo`) kills one latched rat regardless of facing (one per swing, `_quito_en`). Rolling doesn't; blocking makes it bounce off.
  - They join the `"lombrices"` group and answer its calls (`golpe_cuerpo`, `query_ray_hit`, `bullet_hit`, `apply_explosion_impulse`), so the machete, the stab, the spin slam and the pistol reach them without touching `player.gd` (JJ's). Latched rats ignore bullets. The player's origin is its capsule centre: `PECHO`/`PIES` are relative to it.
  - All numbers are first guesses for `docs/pruebas-balance.md`.
- **Viewer**: `godot --path . tools/ver_cucarrata.tscn` (1-6 clips, 7 pare). `-- --fotos <dir>`, `-- --crias <dir>`, and Movie Maker `--write-movie x.avi --fixed-fps 30 -- --video [--solo-pare]`; convert with GStreamer (`openh264enc`, no ffmpeg on JJ's PC).
- **Safehouse**: `.` drops the cucarrata 18 m ahead (a bit to the side, the player hides it otherwise) and makes it give birth to 9 rats; `.` again gives 4 cockroaches, and so on, alternating.
