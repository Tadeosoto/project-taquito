# La cucarrata (`scripts/cucarrata/`, JJ's boss, design in progress, 2026-11-17)

A giant cockroach with a rat's head and blue Serenol eyes, built in code from two Sketchfab models (CC-BY, in `CREDITS.md`): `models/personajes/cucarrata/cucaracha.glb` (skeleton + 6 clips) and `rata.glb` (static mesh). No lore entry, mission or arena yet; the fight's phases are in `JefeCucarrata.gd`.

- **`Cucarrata.gd`**: removes the cockroach's head triangles (vertices weighted mostly to `head_04`; `preparar_cucaracha`), cuts the rat's head (`RATA_CORTE_*`), caps the neck hole (`_tapar_cuello`) and hangs it from `head_04` with a `BoneAttachment3D`. The glb's body is flat (2.2× wider than tall), so `_engordar` stretches the body bones' vertices vertically by `GORDURA` (2.1) around an axis near the belly (`GORDURA_EJE`), blended by body weight so the leg roots stay on; the wings rise with the back (JJ: fatter, more cylindrical; the crías share it). `animar(corto)` with `ANIMS` names (quieta, camina, ataca, muere, vuela, agarra, pare).
- **Skin**: `shaders/cucarrata.gdshader`, the Madre del Cárcamo's keloids and cracks (`madre.gdshader`) adapted to a skinned mesh: cells on the UV (object space would slide while animating), breathing on the global `TIME` at the Madre's 0.25 Hz. Body brown (`CUERPO_COLOR`), rat head stone grey like the Panzón. Antennae (`horns_*` bones) are marked in vertex `COLOR` (g = antenna, r = base→tip) and glow in segments toward the tip (`antena_*` uniforms).
- **`parir(tipos)`**: plays "pare", built in code over "idle" (`_armar_pare`: the abdomen bone `body_02.001_036` rises on its local −X, swells and pushes at `PARE_PUJOS`) and releases a swarm: `POR_PUJO[tipo]` (5 rats, 4 cockroaches) `CriaCucarrata` of the push's type per push, `ENJAMBRE_CADA` s apart, spread sideways (`ENJAMBRE_ABRE`) with varied speeds, from `punta_cola()` into the cucarrata's parent, with `piso_y()` as their floor (`JefeCucarrata` returns its `_piso`: passing its own height while flying made every cockroach dive explode on its own as it dropped below that height). Each birth is one type only and they alternate (JJ: you can't defend against both at once): `parir()` with no list births a `TANDA` of `siguiente_tipo` (3 pushes of rats = 15, or 2 of cockroaches = 8) and flips it. Signal `pario`. Size: `alto` (2 m without antennae; JJ first asked for 3 m, then found it too big in the fight) sets the scale over `ALTO_MODELO` 0.58 m; the eye lights' range is scaled by hand.
- **`CriaCucarrata.gd`**, children with their own crack cells (a scaled-down boss would have millimetre cracks): rats chihuahua-sized, cockroaches about 0.7 m (JJ: first ~1.35 m to be easier to shoot, then half that size but twice as many; hits use `centro()` and `RADIO_CUCARACHA`):
  - Cucaracha (brown): kamikaze. Flies horizontal, head first (the glb's flying pose already is), they fly around the player like flies (JJ: what's scary is seeing them come at you): each heads for a point on the other side of the player (`_nueva_meta`: `MOSCA_RADIO`, `MOSCA_ALTO`, up to `MOSCA_ABRE` off straight across) with limited steering (`MOSCA_GIRA`), so they curve past and cross in front of you, and picks a new one on arrival or after `MOSCA_CAMBIA` s; for `ENJAMBRE_ESPERA` s (JJ: the swarm should be seen flying a while before the kamikaze) nobody attacks, then, from `TURNO_DESDE` m away, and attacks one at a time (`ATACAN_JUNTAS` 1, `ATAQUE_CADA` s after the previous one ends; static `_atacando`): when its turn comes it brakes and hangs in the air for `APUNTA_DURA` while pitching +90° on X (head up, belly forward; −90° turned it upside down, back first) to show the player its belly (the tell to roll), then accelerates belly-first at the player's chest with limited steering (`PICADA_GIRA`), and `explotar()`s in Serenol blue (`_estallido`, bright on purpose (JJ): light-blue core, deep blue shock sphere, blue spark particles and a strong light, all emission above 1 so glow catches it; plus `saco_revienta`) for `EXPLOTA_DANO` within `EXPLOTA_RADIO` through `Player.recibir_dano` (rolling dodges, blocking reduces).
  - Rata (grey): scurries like a fly on the floor around the player (JJ): heads for a point on the other side (`RATA_RADIO`, `RATA_RAPIDEZ`, limited turn `RATA_GIRA`), crossing between your feet, and picks another on arrival or after `RATA_CAMBIA` s. They leap one at a time (static `_ratas_atacando`, `RATA_CADA` s apart, after `RATA_ESPERA` s alive, from `RATA_TURNO` m), bite (`MORDIDA`) and latches at a spot in the player's `frijol` space, slowing (`Player.lastres["cucarrata_ratas"]`) and draining `CHUPA`/s. Only the machete removes it: every swing (`golpe_cuerpo`) counts as a hit on one latched rat regardless of facing (one per swing, `_quito_en`). Rolling doesn't; blocking makes it bounce off.
  - They join the `"lombrices"` group and answer its calls (`golpe_cuerpo`, `query_ray_hit`, `bullet_hit`, `apply_explosion_impulse`), so the machete, the stab, the spin slam and the pistol reach them without touching `player.gd` (JJ's). Latched rats ignore bullets. The player's origin is its capsule centre: `PECHO`/`PIES` are relative to it.
  - All numbers are first guesses for `docs/pruebas-balance.md`.
- **`JefeCucarrata.gd`** (extends `Cucarrata`): the fight, three phases by remaining `vida` (`VIDA` 1200, thirds; JJ's design; it was 400 and phase 1 ended before you could see it):
  1. Walks around the player (`CAMINA_*`, circling with random turns, `_sentido` flips). A bullet (`bullet_hit` → `_enojar`, `ENOJO_ENFRIA` cooldown) or getting within `CERCA` of its mouth makes it growl (`GRITA`: the rat head tilts up and shakes in `_mover_cabeza`, "ataca" clip, `rugido` + low `chillido`), leap so its mouth lands on you (`SALTA`, `SALTO_*`), bite (`MORDIDA` from `BOCA` within `MORDIDA_ALCANCE`; rolling dodges) and run off (`CORRE`). It also stops and gives birth every `PARE_CADA` s (first after `PARE_PRIMERO`) while fewer than `CRIAS_MAX` crías live (`_parir_quieta`: 15 rats only; JJ: the flying cockroach swarm only comes once she flies). JJ reordered the phases: rat births in phase 1, flight (cockroaches) in 2, the stinger in 3.
  2. Takes off (`DESPEGA`; `_despegar` cancels a half-done ground birth) and flies like a slower fly (`VUELO_*`, points across the player, overhead). In the air it pumps flying cockroaches almost nonstop (`parir([CUCARACHA], false)` every `AIRE_PARE_CADA` s while fewer than `AIRE_CRIAS_MAX` live: no "pare" clip, the wings keep going). Bullets barely hurt it (`DANO_BALA_AIRE`), but `TUMBAR_BALAS` of them (an explosion counts `TUMBAR_EXPLOSION`) make it fall on its back (`TUMBA`: roll to PI, `_roll` in the basis; the origin rises `TUMBADA_ALTO` so the back rests on the floor) and kick its legs (`PATAS`: "camina" at `PATALEA` speed, rocking). On its back it does nothing else (JJ): `_tumbar` calls `cancelar_parto()`, which stops a half-done air birth (`parir` checks the `_parto` counter between crías; death cancels too). Then the machete (or an explosion) takes real life, up to `TUMBADA_VIDA` (1/6) per knockdown, so each flying phase takes about two knockdowns (more if you don't hit enough). After `TUMBADA_DURA` s, or when that cap is spent, it flips over (`VOLTEA`, a hop rolling the same way), births 15 rats on the ground (`_parir_y_despegar`) and takes off again. Killed while on its back, it stays on its back (no "muere" clip, which flips it). `_golpe_aire` routes hits in phases 2 and 3.
  3. Same as 2, plus the stinger: every `AGUIJON_CADA` s in flight it goes to `APUNTA_DIST`/`APUNTA_ALTO`, pitches +90° (head up, stinger down) for `APUNTA_DURA` (the tell), then dives stinger-first (`PICADA_*`, `AGUIJON` when the stinger tip is within `AGUIJON_ALCANCE` of the chest) and climbs back (`SUBE`).
  - The stinger (`_poner_aguijon`) is a dark cone with a Serenol-blue tip and light on the abdomen bone. Hits: `CUERPO` spheres along the body (`query_ray_hit`, `golpe_cuerpo` with the blow's damage, `apply_explosion_impulse`); `DANO_BALA` per bullet on the ground. Death kills every cría; in the air it falls first (`CAE`). Signals `murio`, `cambio_fase`. `BarraCucarrata` is its HUD bar (thirds marked, phase name).
  - Crías: both die from `GOLPES` (3) hits, machete swings and bullets counting alike (`_golpe`; JJ, 2026-11-17: dying at one touch they were no threat). Explosions still kill cockroaches at once.
